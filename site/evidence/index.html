Evidence behind the claims

This page used to exist, and it was taken down on purpose. It was a correct, complete, automatically generated record — written in the project's own internal vocabulary, and close to unreadable for anyone who does not maintain it.

What was wrong with it

It opened by telling you that a claim's status is "computed from the levels of the named atoms the claim rests on", and then cited file paths inside the code repository. Those sentences are accurate. They are also no use to a reader trying to work out whether to believe anything on this site, which is the only reason the page exists.

What this page will show

  • For each claim the site makes, the specific thing that would have to be true for it to hold — in plain words, not internal identifiers.
  • What was actually checked, when, and by what — including the checks that failed.
  • Where the evidence is thin or missing, said plainly rather than shown as a tidy blank.
  • A route to the underlying record for anyone who does want the machine-level detail.

Roughly when

Next, alongside the other two pages being rebuilt. The underlying data is unaffected and is still generated and checked on every publish — 6 claim areas, 0 supporting records — so what is missing is a version of it written for a reader, not the evidence itself.

Why a hole rather than the old page. A page that looks thorough and cannot be read is worse than an absence: it invites you to assume it says something it does not. Meanwhile Proof carries the corrections record and the known limitations in readable form.
