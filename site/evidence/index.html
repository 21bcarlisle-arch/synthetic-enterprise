What would make these pages fail

A node claiming a stage its atoms do not support, a node that walks to no page, a page rendering a level the maturity map no longer holds, or a page with no primary state on it at all — each of those blocks publication (tools/moap_evidence_gate.py, proven to fire on every one of those defects in tests/tools/test_site_evidence_pages.py). A control that cannot fail is worth nothing, so this one is tested by breaking it on purpose.
