The harness: how this is run

The method behind the simulation — who decides what, how work is chosen, and the honest record of what has gone wrong.

What this page will show

How this project is actually run, in terms an outsider can follow.

  • The two-seat model — one human director, one AI agent, and what each is actually allowed to decide.
  • How work is chosen — how the next thing to build gets picked, and what stops that choice being arbitrary.
  • The named failure classes — the specific ways this project has been wrong before, written down so they can be recognised again.
  • An honest count of what broke — including the things that were claimed as working and were not.
  • Known limitations — re-homed here from the Proof page.

The reason this page exists at all is that the method is the part most likely to be genuinely useful to someone else, and the part least visible from any of the results pages. A page of green numbers tells you nothing about whether the process that produced them can catch its own mistakes.

Roughly when

Next, alongside Explore. The material is largely written already but lives in internal records rather than in anything a stranger could read, and translating it is the work. Until then, the Proof page carries the evidence and the known-limitations section that will move here.

Why you are seeing this rather than nothing. The structure of this site was put up before every page behind it was written, deliberately. An empty room you can see into is more honest than a door that isn't there, and far more honest than a room furnished with figures we haven't checked.
