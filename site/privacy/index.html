Privacy Policy

How Poesys would handle the personal data of the households and businesses it supplies energy to — the lawful basis, what is collected, how long it is kept, and the rights a customer has.

This is a simulation. Poesys is a high-fidelity simulated UK energy supplier — it is built and run by AI, directed by a human, against real half-hourly settlement data. It has no real customers and processes no real personal data. This policy is a PROVISIONAL reference artefact modelling what a real UK supplier's privacy notice must cover under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. It makes no legal representation to any real data subject and creates no real-world obligation. If you reached this page expecting to manage a real energy account, there isn't one.

1. Who we are

Poesys is a simulated domestic and small-business energy supplier. In this simulation the supplier would be the data controller for the personal data of its customers. Because no real customers exist, there is no real controller and no live processing.

2. What personal data we would collect

A real UK supplier collects only what it needs to supply energy and bill for it. The modelled categories are:

  • Identity & contact — name, supply address, email, phone.
  • Account & supply — MPAN/MPRN, meter serial, tariff, meter reads and half-hourly consumption where a smart meter is used at the customer's chosen granularity.
  • Billing & payment — balance, payment method (e.g. Direct Debit mandate details), payment history.
  • Service & vulnerability — contact history and, only with consent, Priority Services Register needs (e.g. medical dependence on electricity).

3. Lawful basis

Each purpose is tied to a lawful basis under UK GDPR Article 6 (and Article 9 for any special-category data such as health needs on the Priority Services Register):

  • Contract — supplying energy, billing, and managing the account.
  • Legal obligation — settlement, metering, and regulatory reporting duties (Elexon/BSC, Ofgem, HMRC).
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention, network security, and service improvement, balanced against the customer's rights.
  • Consent — marketing, and recording special-category vulnerability needs. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

4. How long we would keep it

Personal data would be retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, then deleted or anonymised. Billing and settlement records are typically kept for six years to meet tax and regulatory obligations; marketing consent lapses when withdrawn.

5. Who we would share it with

Data would be shared only where necessary to supply energy: the network operators and the central settlement system (Elexon/BSC), the meter operator, payment processors, and regulators or authorities where the law requires it. Data would not be sold.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR a customer has the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the processing of their personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In this simulation there is no personal data to exercise these rights over.

7. Contact

In a live supplier this section would name the Data Protection Officer and a contact route. As a simulation, Poesys has no customer-facing contact channel and holds no personal data. Poesys is built and run by AI, directed by a human.
