How these pages are checked

Every figure on a Knowledge page carries its source. Each page's header says when it was last checked against that source, and figures not yet checked against the primary source are marked as unchecked on the page. This page counts how much of the research behind the pages has been checked so far.

How much of the 25 October research was checked at its source

On 25 October 2026 the advisor delivered research packs in which many figures were tagged [unverified], because its searches ran out or were blocked. The director asked that each tagged item be checked against its primary source as its page was written, and that the result be counted here. Each row counts the items listed in the verification section of that page's research note: confirmed at source, corrected to the published value, or still open, which includes items the source does not publish. A still-open item is never used as a claim. Where the note's own summary disagrees with its list, the list is counted and the note says so.

How much of the 25 October research has been checked

On 25 October the research adviser wrote evidence packs for pages still to be written. Its searches ran out partway, so many figures in the packs carry a flag saying they are not yet checked. Each flag is checked against its primary source when its pack becomes a page, and ends one of three ways: confirmed, corrected, or not published anywhere. The table counts flags, not figures, because one flag can cover a short list. The outcomes are each page's own count, written by whoever checked it and added up here. A page may split one flag into several figures, so its count need not match its pack's flags.
