What the method has shown

Poesys is a method for finding the customers it can create value for. This page is that method's own record: each question it has put to its simulated world, and what came back. Results that went against us stay here, and so do the ones the evidence cannot yet settle. These are our own interpretations of our own experiments, so they are insight, not knowledge, and any of them may be wrong.

What each status means
  • Held: a prediction written down before the run, and the run did not refute it.
  • Did not hold: a prediction written down before the run, and the run refuted it.
  • Withdrawn: something we published and later took back, kept with the reason.
  • Measured: a result with no prediction written down before it. It describes; it does not confirm.
  • We cannot tell: the evidence does not settle it either way, and the record says so.
The improvement loop
