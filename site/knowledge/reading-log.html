The reading log

What we have read about building software, products and AI agents, and what we did about each source. Every entry says what the source claims, how strong its evidence is, our verdict and what changed here because of it. Sources we rejected stay in the log, with the reason, so the same argument is not had twice. Market and household sources are not here; they are the other Knowledge pages.

Where each entry sits on the map

Evidence is graded from weakest to strongest: a vendor post, a single author, a book, a peer-reviewed paper, data from many organisations. Most of what reaches us is one of the first two, and the verdicts are weighed with that in mind. Reading a source never adds a new check on its own: a recommendation from it is ranked with the rest of the work and must name the problem it fixes.
