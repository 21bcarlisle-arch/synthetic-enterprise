What we think, and how sure we are

The Knowledge pages hold two different things. Knowledge is what outside sources say, or what we work out from outside data by a named method. Insight is ours: an interpretation, a synthesis or a hypothesis. It may be wrong, so every insight here says how far it has been tested, what would show it wrong, and when it is next checked. An insight that has been shown wrong stays on this page, struck through, with what refuted it. Some insights come from our own simulation; those are marked, and none of them may set or calibrate the simulated world.

Filter by any facet

Every page carries five facets: what it is about (domain), whether it is knowledge or insight (layer), who could know it (side), how sure we are (trust), and what reads it (use). Pick any combination; the address bar keeps it, so a filtered view can be linked.
