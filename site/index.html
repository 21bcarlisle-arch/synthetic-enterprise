An energy company built and run by AI, to find the cheapest tonne of carbon left.

No customers. No licence. A running simulator -- a fully autonomous licensed supplier will not be permitted, because a licence needs accountable humans, and we say so up front. Autonomy is the how, not the headline.

Not a model of a company; a running one. It settles half-hourly against real Elexon and NESO market data since 2016, takes only proven answers toward the real world, and discovers its world the way a real supplier would -- meter reads, market feeds, customer contacts, bills, regulatory publications -- never the simulation's own internals. The mission is carbon abatement through personalisation, measured in £ per tonne of CO₂ saved.

The score: £ per tonne of CO₂e abated NOT YET MEASURED

The claim, falsifiable: that you cut carbon fastest by knowing each household well — shifting when it draws power, fitting the right assets, pricing the right tariff, having the right conversation — run as a real autonomous supplier inside a faithful simulation of the actual UK market. Every line of company code has to answer one question: could a real supplier know this?

The score is £ per tonne of CO₂e saved, measured against the yardstick it has to beat — the UK government's carbon appraisal value, £273/tCO₂e (2025).

The honest state, plainly: the carbon ledger is designed, not yet instrumented — no number is shown here until each household's half-hourly meter reads meet real grid carbon intensity. When it runs, the trajectory appears here first. The economics that would fund it — the true cost-to-serve gap — are worked through on Proof.

The wallLive

The company never sees inside the simulation. It learns its world only the way a real supplier would — market feeds, forecasts that carry error, imperfect meter reads, payments, complaints — and the settled truth about a period lands months after it happened.

The companyBuilding

One joined-up stack: win customers, forecast the book, price tariffs, hedge forward, bill on three clocks, chase what’s owed, settle late and learn from the true-up. Billing, collections and the discovery loop run today; cost-to-serve, competitors and the cash-collateral death-loop are still to come. It can be wrong, and it can die.

The scoreBuilding

Three tests: survive a worst-case shock, earn on a probability-weighted basis, and abate — cut carbon per household, priced in £ per tonne. Survive and earn run today; the carbon meter is designed but not yet wired to live meter reads, and the site says so rather than pretend.
