## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**1. The mission, value created then shared, the method as the asset.** "We are creating enterprise value by automating ways to find individual customers we can create value for, and sharing in that value — by saving them money, time and carbon, through personalised modelling, tariffs and advice." "Value is created and THEN shared, so every decision has two sides. Transfer is not creation." "The enterprise value is the automated METHOD of finding those customers, not the book." The mission is the director's alone: "The reservation is narrow: director documents and the mission statement"; a change of intent goes to staging as a proposal, a marked factual correction lands.
sources: R-C1-93, R-C2-31, R-C2-32, R-S-1, R-S-2, R-S-4, R-S-99 (2026-08-28); R-L-28 (2026-10-04)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md yes; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**2. Sharing is not transfer.** "cost is not loaded onto a group because it is less able or less likely to push back." A save offer on a loss notice may vary by acquisition route or time on the default tariff; renewal prices may not; "a save offer must never be paid for by raising the price of customers who stay"; vulnerable customers "at least as well as anyone else in the same position".
sources: R-L-49 (2026-10-08)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**3. Three currencies; time is effort.** "money, time and carbon"; his shorthand is "cash, carbon and convenience". Ceilings are stated "in physical units first" and monetised on top; "Effort is the 'time' currency". Open proposal: time in household minutes, "never converted to GBP".
sources: R-C1-93, R-S-3, R-S-103 (2026-08-28); R-D-59 (2026-09-05); R-S-154 (2026-10-04, open); R-L-38 (2026-10-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (three words yes; convenience, effort, minutes no); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**4. Carbon's job.** "Its main job is to show customers what they've saved and could save in CO2, so those motivated by it can enjoy it. That clarifies the mission's carbon part rather than changing it." Test, not accept: heating "may be around five times the carbon of all its electricity". Map measures win-win, carbon-led or money-led, "the basis for next best action". History comes from NESO's published series: "a correlated estimate — it doesn't need to be precise."
sources: R-L-36 (2026-10-04), R-L-38 (2026-10-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (NESO only as a data source); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**5. The thesis.** Decide "customer-by-customer on lifetime value — using only what it can actually know": "like an average player by default", beating average "precisely to the degree it understands and predicts the truth behind the SIM better than average", "through better prediction and nothing else"; "from inference, never from access". Personalisation pays where averaging "disappoints and undervalues"; otherwise it is "always a segment, never a person".
sources: R-S-123 (undated, June 2026), R-C1-82 (2026-08-25), R-F-25 (2026-08-26), R-C2-31 (2026-08-28), R-D-36 (2026-09-07)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (extracts say partly; grep finds no CLV, lifetime value or customer-by-customer wording); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**6. How the thesis is judged.** "The fair test compares decisions, not books": per customer at each renewal, what each rule "would have earned on that customer, at that moment"; the per-decision probe is "nine times the signal at a tenth of the cost". Payment history is "the first rung of my CLV merit order". Honest negatives on the right population beat "a £3M headline on the wrong one". "I don't care about profit — it's fiction"; "a household that couldn't exist" is the failure. Attribution can be "scored against hidden truth".
sources: R-C1-68 (2026-08-24), R-F-31 (2026-08-26), R-D-69 (2026-09-06), R-L-22, R-L-25 (fenced paste), R-L-26 (2026-10-03)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**7. Payment, credit risk and forward cash forecasting are "the heart of this business."** One engine on "a forward cash forecast per customer: expected bills from the one consumption forecast, expected payments and their probability given payment method and track record". It drives the provision (expected loss replacing the flat assumption), balance limits, the personal payment plan, early warnings and self-serve responses; "Human handling is kept for vulnerability and exceptions." "Credit risk sets how far a customer's balance may drift from zero, not how much they pay over the year." One consumption forecast replaces four.
sources: R-L-22, R-L-26 (2026-10-03); R-L-59, R-L-61 (2026-10-11); R-L-64 (2026-10-12)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**8. Next best action.** "tailored next best action per customer — their benefit first, then ours"; debt management is "a form of next best action"; judged by CLV "rather than as a fixed ranking of measures". Unpublished truth becomes an assumption toggle with a range; bring it to him only if "decision-changing and unresolvable."
sources: R-L-37, R-L-38, R-L-39 (2026-10-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**9. The world/supplier line.** "The world creates customer behaviour behind the curtain, from evidence. The supplier sees only what a real supplier could see, and problem-solves against it. The supplier's value is in closing that gap, so it must not be designed from knowledge of what's behind the curtain." Four principles: (1) World first, built from research "with no reference to any supplier policy". (2) Freeze, then solve: file the world's predictions before any supplier test; no change after seeing supplier results "unless new evidence requires it, and say so". (3) Separate lanes; the supplier reads only through the seam; graded on fresh seeds across uncertainty ranges. (4) "Truth stays an instrument": probe and holdout truth are "for grading only, never inputs to a supplier decision". The four-principles audit is pending. Test on every line: "could a real supplier know this?"
sources: R-B-71 (2026-07-23, the test only), R-L-62 (2026-10-11), R-L-64 (2026-10-12)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (wall and seam only); DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (§1 wall)

**10. Fidelity serves the mission; the world must be able to defeat it.** "Fidelity is not the goal — it is the precondition: a world that cannot press back cannot tell value created from value transferred." "World fidelity work continues, but only where it blocks a supplier result." Roles are "answers to physics". "It can be wrong, and it can die." Survival is judged on the domestic book.
sources: R-B-72, R-B-100 (2026-07-23), R-B-113 (2026-07-25), R-C1-93 (2026-08-28), R-L-57 (2026-10-10)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (precondition yes; domestic book and supplier priority no); DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (R12 only)

**11. The route to reality is narrow.** "we need to remember a hard time [sic] that you cannot spend any real money. Simulation is different to real money of mine." A fully autonomous licensed supplier "is not something the regulator will permit"; "The capped-human design is a research condition, not a licensing plan." Real households only through consenting volunteers with a parallel bill, "including where we were wrong", after a security posture review. Seeking, in order: practitioner stress-testing, volunteers, routes to households, sponsorship.
sources: R-A-8, R-A-14, R-A-16 (2026-07-28); R-L-08 (2026-09-27)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (real money, real customer or market yes; regulator, volunteers no); DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (§3 items 1 and 7 only)

**12. Product brief.** "Inertia and reluctance ... are not obstacles. They are the operating conditions": ask "for the smallest change", never "the same thing twice from someone who has already declined". "Moments of action, not campaigns"; the test is "did this household's cost and carbon actually bend, or did we merely send a message?" Business customers and global-by-design are design properties: "Britain first."
sources: R-A-12, R-A-13, R-A-15 (2026-07-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**13. Endgame, and the project's own claim.** The Epoch-4 fitness function and mortality rule are the director's alone; the tournament is "where EV is fitness and mortality is selection" (MOAP). "The claim must be the PROJECT's, not the simulated company's": can software be grown rather than written (R-C2-42); "Simulate the real system first" (R-S-118). Standing status of the last two is disputed (section 3).
sources: R-S-118 (undated, June 2026), R-E-18 (2026-07-12), R-B-100 (2026-07-23), R-C2-42 (2026-08-17)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (§3 item 6 and §6 hold the fitness reservation; nothing else)

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. Commercial or not. R-A-5 (decision 2026-07-21): "non-commercial ... public domain as the plan". R-C1-93 (2026-08-28): "creating enterprise value ... sharing in that value". A ruling would need to decide whether the non-commercial and public-domain terms are withdrawn or still bound the mission.
2. Third currency. "time" (R-C1-93, 2026-08-28); effort (R-D-59, 2026-09-05); minutes, never GBP (R-S-154, 2026-10-04, open); "convenience" (R-L-38, 2026-10-05). A ruling would need to decide which it is, and which words stand on CLAUDE.md and the MOAP.
3. Carbon as headline. MOAP Timeframe 2 "Carbon becomes the headline ... £/tCO₂e on the front page" (R-B-72, 2026-07-23; "survives intact", R-S-99, 2026-08-28) against carbon's main job as customer-facing saving (R-L-38, 2026-10-05). A ruling would need to decide whether £/tCO₂e stays the public headline.
4. Next-best-action key. Every decision has two sides (R-S-2, 2026-08-28); "their benefit first, then ours", yet "judged by CLV", which is our value (R-L-39, 2026-10-05). The c34 row, open since 2026-10-06, still ranks on supplier value alone (R-S-2 note). A ruling would need to decide whether household benefit is a first priority, a floor or a weighted term.
5. Fitness. Epoch-4 fitness and mortality are the director's alone and undecided (R-E-18, 2026-07-12); MOAP Endgame says "EV is fitness" (R-B-72, 2026-07-23); enterprise value is the method, not the book (R-S-4, 2026-08-28). A ruling would need to decide what the tournament selects on.
6. The front door's claim. Autopoiesis, "not the simulated company's" (R-C2-42, 2026-08-17); the mission on CLAUDE.md and the MOAP (R-C1-93, R-S-99, 2026-08-28); a new software method in the pitch (R-S-118). The seat reads them as nested (R-C2-31 note), unratified. A ruling would need to say which sentence the front door carries.
7. Go-live. The pitch's Transition Test (R-S-118 source, June 2026): five conditions, then swapping to real interfaces "is an integration task". Against it: "a research condition, not a licensing plan" (R-A-14, 2026-07-28) and no real money (R-L-08, 2026-09-27). No extract rules on go-live; the Test is in no page but pitch.md (grep). A ruling would need to retire it, keep it as a milestone test, or replace it with R-A-16's list.
