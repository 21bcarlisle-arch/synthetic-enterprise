## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**Price is formed, never asserted or fitted.** "I really want pricing to be right in the end, not just look right now." Power must be "substantially reconstructible from gas, carbon, demand and wind on ordinary days — or it was not formed, only generated." "The retail prices we expect must be driven by this data, not asserted beside it."
sources: R-B-61 (2026-07-22), R-A-82 (2026-07-25)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (R12 covers only the anti-fitting half); MOAP no

**Every decision has two sides; the household share is measured, not scored.** `test_household_share_is_not_yet_a_target.py` bars it from every organ; releasing the two-sided objective is "R13's and yours". For next best action: "their benefit first, then ours."
sources: R-C2-35 (2026-08-28), R-L-39 (2026-10-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly ("Transfer is not creation"); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP yes

**Margin stays off the front door; no value-based pricing at quote.** "Margin stays off the front door — leave it as you've set it", checks a reader cannot grade stay "reported-not-blocking". "Value-based acquisition spend and service design, not value-based pricing at quote."
sources: R-C1-49 (2026-08-20), R-D-71 (2026-09-06)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**Cost is not loaded onto a group because it is less able or less likely to push back.** "Turning customers down on payment behaviour, credit or arrears is ordinary practice, but shifting cost onto prepayment customers isn't." This corrects the earlier paraphrase, "a price keyed to the meter may not", which is to be fixed "wherever it's recorded".
sources: R-L-49 §3 (2026-10-08 08:52)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no (paraphrase not found on any of the three)

**Retention is save and win-back, not blanket discounts.** "GB retention is mainly save and win-back activity on the loss notice, plus retention of home movers. Blanket renewal discounts are not the lever." "A save offer made on a loss notice may vary by acquisition route or time on the default tariff. Renewal prices may not." Condition: "a save offer must never be paid for by raising the price of customers who stay", shown by checking stayers' prices with and without save offers. SLC 22B (tariffs open to new and existing customers alike) and its retention carve-out are to be checked as still in force, with any other current GB rule on save or retention offers; no answer is logged.
sources: R-L-48 §3 (2026-10-08 06:46), R-L-49 §1, §3 (08:52)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**Protection is a floor outside the ranking; vulnerable customers fare at least as well as anyone.** "No metric — learning-value included — can ever demote a regulatorily-significant group (vulnerability, fuel poverty, prepayment, priority-services, debt) below full-fidelity modelling. Protection is a floor outside the ranking, not a term inside it." "Vulnerable customers must be treated at least as well as anyone else in the same position." Location is opt-in and "never set back on inference alone for anyone who may be vulnerable"; gamification "not regressive". Harm "must SCALE WITH VULNERABILITY". Vulnerable and stretched households are reported separately; "human handling is kept for vulnerability and exceptions." The 18°C health floor ("don't price comfort — floor it") is parked, not revoked (R-F-113, R-F-114).
sources: R-E-36 (2026-07-13), R-A-98 (2026-07-20), R-D-71 (2026-09-06), R-F-113 (2026-09-08), R-L-49 (2026-10-08), R-L-59 (2026-10-11 04:20), R-L-61 (2026-10-11 07:41)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no (archetype mention only)

**Pricing follows expected cost, and the rules come from sources, not recollection.** "Default risk, collections cost and bad debt are part of that cost. Put them inside the EV arithmetic and let the answer emerge rather than imposing a floor." The register of what is permitted, prohibited and tolerated-but-unwritten, with citations, "is what the pricing arm should be constrained by — not my recollection and not yours." "The pricing decision is blind to payment history": forward arrears and credit risk, "let arrears move the bad-debt cost as well as churn". "When a question touches regulation, search Ofgem's licence conditions and decisions before asking me." Knowledge page first, then build, the live save included.
sources: R-C1-84 (2026-08-25), R-L-22 (2026-10-03 00:48), R-L-26 (11:46), R-L-59 §1 (2026-10-11 04:20), R-L-60 (04:21)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (knowledge-first adjacent); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (commons doctrine adjacent); MOAP no

**Ability to pay is a hard constraint; a fixed DD rests on current information.** A repayment mechanism "without an affordability assessment is non-compliant by construction" (SLC 27.5–27.8). Ofgem's 2022 decision strengthened SLC 27.15: a fixed DD "must be set on the best and most current information in all cases ... and reassessed when new information would change it materially."
sources: R-B-131 (2026-07-25), R-L-59 §1 (2026-10-11 04:20)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (line 14); MOAP no

**Payment plan and debt are one engine on a forward cash forecast.** "Credit risk sets how far a customer's balance may drift from zero, not how much they pay over the year. Low-risk customers get smoothing, prompt refund of surplus credit and no catch-up shocks. Higher-risk customers are kept near zero without winter bill shock." Provision becomes expected loss per customer. Automated self-serve help (lower payment, breathing space, repayment plan) sits "within those limits"; supplier-controlled levers first, customer-chosen options only as take-up sensitivities; "keep the portfolio ceiling on credit held".
sources: R-L-59 §3, §5 (2026-10-11 04:20), R-L-61 (07:41), R-L-64 (2026-10-12)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (bill shock defined, lines 84-86); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**Harm is priced, bounded and signed.** "R = 8:1 SIGNED. Band 5:1–10:1", changed only by his explicit re-signature. Harm is heavy-tailed; the £90m British Gas aggregate anchors it; "licence-loss as a SCENARIO, not a probability." Hedging stays "within a director-set risk envelope"; the 2021-style tail must be "survivable by construction".
sources: R-E-35, R-E-36 (both 2026-07-13), R-D-71 (2026-09-06)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no by grep (extracts say partly, via the baseline/curriculum wall); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**What serves the customer is not always what the market has normalised.** "The gap between true need and market norm is itself a modellable phenomenon and arguably where the product opportunity sits." Registered as a candidate personalisation product.
sources: R-B-126 (2026-07-25)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. Two-sided score. R-C2-35 (2026-08-28): household share measured, not scored, release "yours". R-L-39 (2026-10-05): "their benefit first, then ours", next best action "judged by CLV". A ruling would need to decide whether household benefit is now a gate or scored term, or whether R-C2-35's release is still pending.
2. Renewal price and payment history. R-L-22 and R-L-26 (2026-10-03): forward arrears and credit risk into the renewal margin, "pricing changes are allowed". R-L-49 §3 (2026-10-08): renewal prices may not vary by route or time on default, and cost is not loaded onto groups that cannot push back. A ruling would need to decide whether payment-history renewal pricing is cost-reflective or cost-loading.
3. Pre-expiry retention offer. R-L-49 §1 asks for a test of "a retention fix offered to customers nearing the end of their term"; R-L-49 §3 lets save offers vary but not renewal prices, with SLC 22B unchecked. A ruling would need to decide which class a pre-expiry fix belongs to.
4. Money representation. R-B-167 (2026-07-28) reserves float versus Decimal to the director ("The ruling is his"; no migration on the machine's initiative), against the Tier-1 rule "bills accurate above all" with every sampled module in float. A ruling would need to decide whether to migrate at a boundary or accept float with a measured drift bound.
5. Note on sources, not a conflict. R-L-48 §3 as logged is the retention answer; the 23 Sept ruling is outside the log window (starts 25 Sept) and appears only as restated in R-L-49 §3.
