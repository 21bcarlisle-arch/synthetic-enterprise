## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

1. **The budget is the design.** "Human input, money, tokens and carbon are all deliberately capped … capped human input forces genuine autonomy; capped cost forces architectural efficiency; … capped carbon forces the delivery mechanism to be as light as the outcome it claims."
sources: R-A-9 (2026-07-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

2. **Work is the default; rest is the defect.** "Why does it not always have something to do?" — "it does; it cannot see it; nothing inside it is authorized to mint it." Rest is indefensible "while any published register holds an open item"; a further stall class "is an R10 breach of R17, not a new incident."
sources: R-A-17 (2026-07-19), R-B-39 (07-21), R-B-53 (07-22), R-B-75, R-B-77, R-B-78, R-B-80, R-B-95, R-B-96 (07-23), R-A-31, R-A-32 (07-27), R-B-155 (07-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (R16/R17 defined on neither page)

3. **Ship what a reader can see; judge work by whether it converges on a change.** "Verifying yourself has crowded out shipping"; "fix the block, don't file it"; "open the live site first and last, push on every commit, publishing breaking is stop-the-line, and nothing gets a new control unless it guards something that has shipped." "Make it impossible for the system to run indefinitely on work that cannot change its own state" without "making discovery expensive"; "an hour of looking that ends in two lines is exactly right; a seventh pass that ends in another document is not."
sources: R-C1-29, R-C1-32, R-C1-42, R-C1-43 (2026-08-19), R-C1-62 (08-21), R-C1-73 (08-24)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

4. **Director instructions and severe alarms draw first.** "Director-authored acts are rung-zero, ahead of staged rulings, campaigns, backlog and HARDEN alike"; "Rung 1 means rung 1"; "Routine director acts should approach zero." A severe alarm preempts the draw; "'producer down' [is as] loud as 'publisher wedged.'"
sources: R-B-146 (2026-07-27), R-A-64 (07-29), R-F-1 (08-09), R-F-21 (08-17)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

5. **Every run is accountable.** Rotation is "a STRATIFIED grid over world-scenario × population-seed, never a random pick; randomness lives inside the cell"; every run emits a ledger row, without which "improvement cannot be distinguished from luck"; one canonical RNG substream — "a tournament over lives whose seeding is derived eight different ways is not a tournament"; identical inputs regenerate nothing.
sources: R-E-43 (2026-07-08), R-B-74 (07-23), R-B-110, R-B-111 (07-25), R-B-162 (07-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

6. **Scale posture.** "Preclude nothing at 10⁶ customers; engineer for 10⁵; probe at 10⁴. Seams must survive the design ceiling; internals need only the current rung." "Go-live book ≈ 10³"; smart "≥90%." Building internals ahead of need "remains the ghost-suburb failure"; the product plane "does not exist until Epoch 5" and AWS "is opened at Epoch-5 mobilisation." Sim-time: state moves at decision points only, never half-hourly (stale).
sources: R-S-143 (undated), R-C2-75, R-C2-78, R-C2-82, R-C2-84 (2026-08-07)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

7. **Book size and composition.** "Take the 80 founders" is the live default. "One case stands for many … Two things must remain reversible, and nothing may be built that forecloses them." "Holdout groups are useless at about 80 decisions … the book needs to reach a few thousand customers … The known constraints are ours, not physics." Seed-family runner: "40-founder books settling every win, four side by side on distinct seeds under the box budget" (482 homes, 36 min, 3 GB each). Four practice books each score one skill, "decided blind to P&L." (§3.)
sources: R-C2-30 (2026-08-28), R-D-54 (09-05), R-D-39 (09-07), R-D-48 (10-07), R-S-161 (10-10)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

8. **Two budgets: tokens and RAM.** "Tokens are not the constraint; a tight cap re-invents goal-seeking" — the runaway signature is "sustained turns with zero level-transitions"; "where the real signal is unavailable it should run rather than skip — a false stop costs more than a false start here." RAM: "a real supplier with millions of accounts plainly doesn't work that way … process and write, process and discard"; a cap is "a tripwire that never fires"; memory is "a budget you're spending rather than a problem that was solved"; heavy jobs and housekeeping "declare and defer, never collide," with teeth.
sources: R-E-19 (2026-07-15), R-B-6 (07-19), R-C2-62 (08-10), R-C1-34 (08-19), R-C1-71, R-C1-73 (08-24), R-C1-86 (08-25), R-F-27 (08-26), R-S-61 (08-28), R-C1-110 (08-31), R-F-86 (09-06)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

9. **Serial by default; one writer per tree; widenings fenced.** "Main loop is 1-wide by construction … Width lives INSIDE turns," raised only on measured headroom. "Concurrent work in the shared tree has cost us more than it has bought." A widening is fenced with its date and never self-certified — "That refusal is worth more to me than the tokens." R-B-20/21 (light never waits behind heavy) sit here in tension — §3.
sources: R-E-21 (2026-07-15), R-B-20, R-B-21 (07-19), R-C1-45 (08-19), R-C1-72 (08-24), R-S-61 (08-28), R-F-67 (09-04), R-F-87 (09-06)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

10. **Landing discipline.** "Commit or park whatever the interrupted run left in the tree"; finished work never committed "is the disease that has cost us most"; divergence is "named daily, never punished via the public site"; "verify against origin before saying landed"; never ship red code, touch wall controls or bypass hooks — "only halt for a genuine wall."
sources: R-F-4 (2026-08-09), R-C1-24 (08-18), R-C1-45 (08-19), R-F-33 (08-26), R-D-16 (09-02)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

11. **Publishing breathes, and publishes weekly.** Publishing "requires green on the code that produces and renders the published numbers, nothing else"; last-known-good serves "under a dated banner" — "never a frozen stamp." "The site publishes numbers and runs once a week … The reason is cost," once the liveness signal is reassigned, because "a week-old site saying 'as at Monday' is honest." The settlement ceiling needs "a non-circular basis."
sources: R-C1-130, R-C1-132 (2026-08-10), R-C1-100 (08-29), R-F-69 (09-04)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

12. **Test throughput is a scope constraint, not housekeeping.** "Testing starts to become rate determining and possibly limit the scope of the programme": an hour per validation is unaffordable, minutes make a research instrument — "the director never again waits an hour to learn whether one change was sound." "Measure before restructuring"; tier by cadence, select by impact, surface failures in seconds; "the full suite still runs on a cadence as the net."
sources: R-B-25, R-B-26, R-B-27 (2026-07-19), R-A-101 (07-20), R-C2-60 (08-10), R-S-57 (08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

13. **The seat and its daemons.** "Context isolation, not model foreignness, is the independence mechanism." "Seat destination = an always-on cloud box … one seat on Earth" (unexecuted — §4). "Restart any daemon that does not host the interactive session whenever its loaded code is behind disk … build that as a mechanism rather than remembering it"; "no third state"; an interrupted seat is "recoverable without me noticing it … I shouldn't be the mechanism that spots a stall"; a cold start checks for stranded work.
sources: R-C2-76, R-C2-77 (2026-08-07), R-F-11 (08-13), R-C1-67 (08-24), R-F-65 (09-04), R-F-122 (09-15)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

sources: R-E-23 (2026-07-15), R-A-92 (07-19), R-C2-76 (08-07), R-F-9 (08-12), R-S-61 (08-28)

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. **Parallelism.** R-B-20, R-B-21 (2026-07-19): "I still don't like the move at the pace of the slowest approach" — light work never queues behind a validation run; the fan cap becomes a budget. Against R-C1-72 (08-24) "Do these one at a time, not in parallel" and R-S-61 (08-28) "Serial by default." A ruling would need to say whether serial-by-default governs the draw itself or only writers in the shared tree.

2. **Work is the default vs don't draw what cannot change a level.** R-B-96 (07-23) "rest should be structurally unreachable … Planning is work"; R-B-6 (07-19) spend spare tokens on optional discovery. Against R-C1-73 (08-24) "don't draw work that cannot change a level" and R-F-86 (09-06) "Spend it on stage 1 … Not on machinery." R-C1-62 (08-21) gives the convergence test but not what the machine does when nothing drawable can change a level: plan, discover, or rest.

3. **Who sets cadence, from what signal.** R-E-19 (07-15): the turn ceiling is "Director-set, versioned; never agent-tuned." Against R-F-86 (09-06): "Raise the tick … to whatever your own reading of consumption says … you can [see the burn rate]." R-C1-110 (08-31) found the machine's usage read false; R-F-86's note records the 09-04 rhythm instruction dropping the token cycle as unreadable. A ruling would need to say whether tick cadence is director-set or machine-set, against which verified signal.

4. **Site attention under weekly publishing.** R-C1-43 (08-19): "open the live site first and last"; "publishing breaking is stop-the-line." Against R-F-69 (09-04) weekly numbers at "a cadence nobody reads at" and R-C1-87 (08-25, cited in R-C1-43) "Don't panic about the website. Nobody is reading it." A ruling would need to say which of the five 08-19 commitments survive weekly publishing and what now carries the liveness signal.

5. **Which book-size lever is driving.** R-D-39 (09-07): one case stands for many; raising counts is a reversible lever, "Neither is built now." R-D-48 (10-07): "the book needs to reach a few thousand customers." R-S-161 (10-10): four 40-founder books side by side (~2,000 homes) "under the box budget," with R-C2-30's 80-founder book still the default. A ruling would need to say which lever is driving — four small books pooled, one settled book of thousands (with the box budget giving way), or weighted cases — and whether 80 or 40 founders is the default.
