Director's words in quotation marks.

## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**1. Roles.** "The mission and the direction are mine. Yours is everything between that and the work … When something blocks, you unblock it rather than report it. When priorities conflict, you decide rather than ask. You also own the judgement about what reaches me" — permanently, and interrupting him needlessly is as much a failure as deciding "a change of direction."
sources: R-B-50 2026-07-22; R-B-175 07-29; R-C1-90 08-25; R-F-22 08-26; R-S-6, R-S-63 08-28; R-D-1 09-01
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**2. The Standard.** "stable, autonomous, continuous, parallel velocity" — "Running out of things to do is a failure, not a clean finish", and "where any prior ruling conflicts, this governs."
sources: R-B-50 2026-07-22; R-B-168 07-29
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**3. No one-way doors inside the simulation; reversibility by recording, not asking.** "Nothing here is irreversible — no money, no customers, no licence. Decide, act, record what you chose and what you rejected. That record is what I review, and it replaces asking." The test is "can this be undone by a single act, with no external consequence in the meantime? YES → proceed at risk" — "You are authorised to be wrong."
sources: R-E-17, R-S-93 2026-07-12; R-E-31 07-16; R-A-75, R-A-106, R-B-169, R-B-188, R-B-189 07-29; R-C2-74 08-05; R-C2-54 08-09; R-C1-27 08-18; R-C1-38 08-19; R-C1-79 08-24; R-F-26 08-26; R-C1-104 08-31; R-D-1 09-01; R-F-84 09-05; R-S-26 10-04
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**4. Reserved actions: only real-world consequence outside the simulation.** "spending real money; contacting real people; publishing anything irreversible under Poesys's name; changing safety controls that exist to protect real people" — in August "curriculum values, one-way doors, and anything that spends money or touches a real person"; enumeration unresolved (§3.1).
sources: R-E-12 2026-07-12; R-E-31 07-16; R-A-58, R-A-74, R-B-169, R-B-189 07-29; R-C1-38 08-19; R-C1-53 08-20; R-S-29 10-04
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly

**5. Curriculum values (R13) are the director's, "because they are the science."** "What I reserve is direction, not category: never tune the world to flatter a result, and never choose a curriculum value because of what it does to our numbers" — the genuine choices ("how many households are elastic, how big the population is, how long the window runs") and un-anchorable quantities ("Do not fill them") are his, corrections are made "and tell me", ambiguity is resolved by "Erring against our own thesis", axis verdicts are his, and "Detection is a sensing organ; it does not license an acting organ."

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

**3.1 Which reserved-actions list governs.** DIRECTOR_CANON §3's seven, "complete and fixed until the director changes it" (R-E-12/R-S-88, 2026-07-12); his four external categories (R-A-58/R-A-74/R-B-169, 07-29); CLAUDE.md's five (R-S-29, 10-04). Plainly: the seven are superseded by his own words of 07-29 and by THE_STANDARD's "where any prior ruling conflicts, this governs" (R-B-168) — §3 is stale, not standing. Beyond that the extracts do not show which wording governs: CLAUDE.md's five is what the code enforces but is recorded as the machine's restatement from code, not his ruling (R-S-153); it drops "safety controls that exist to protect real people", adds "real-world commitment" and "live credential", and neither list carries evidence destruction, which he named three times (R-E-12; R-B-189; R-C1-38 note), nor the DNS door (R-C1-53). The extracts' own reading: "the true reserved list is four external categories + curriculum values + the fitness function, and no single page says so" (R-B-169 note); open since 10-04 (R-S-153). A ruling would need to publish one list in his words and say whether evidence destruction, DNS and credentials are on it.

**3.2 The values / fitness-function reservation.** DIRECTOR_CANON item 6, "above all others, this one" (R-E-12, 07-12; R-E-13, 07-13; still open in R-S-94) and MOTHBALL's "do not silently drop it" (07-29, per R-B-169) versus CLAUDE.md, which omits it and routes canon intent as "escalate with a proposal, and don't wait" (R-S-27/R-S-29, 10-04). A ruling would need to say whether "what the company is FOR" is a hard reservation or an escalation that proceeds on silence.

**3.3 Curriculum: how strict, in which words.** "R13 is a WALL, not a dial" (R-C2-37, R-C1-38, 08-19) versus his narrowing to genuine choices with the correction-vs-choice test and the against-our-thesis tie-break (R-F-49 08-30; R-D-24/R-D-25 09-04); the open row says "the curriculum wall reads stricter than your 2026-09-04 canon" (R-S-153). A ruling would need to adopt one text for the wall and name the reserved choices.

**3.4 SE_DRAW_POPULATION.** "Do not treat these ratifications as the activation" (R-B-180, 07-29) versus, the same day, a BUILD_OPEN on a ratified decision "is not a decision; it is plumbing" (R-B-188/R-B-189), activation having been authorised the day before (R-B-151); the flip is never recorded. A ruling would need to say whether switching on a ratified world is plumbing (the seat's) or the choice of which world (his).

**3.5 L3 versus reviewer independence.** External review reserved to his choosing (R-C2-71, 08-05) versus an L3 bar that counts reviewer independence against every atom, so none can reach L3 (R-S-156, 10-04, open). A ruling would need to accept or reject the proposed pass with the limit recorded.
