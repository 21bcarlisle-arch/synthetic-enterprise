# Ofgem, Collective Switch trials: final report (2019)

- **Publisher, vintage:** Ofgem, Behavioural Insights team, 2019. Five randomised controlled trials with three
  suppliers, February 2018 to April 2019. Eligibility: on a default tariff with the supplier for three years or more.
- **Licence:** Crown copyright 2019, Open Government Licence v3.0 (stated in the report).
- **What is here:** the report, raw and unmodified; and `trial_results.csv`, the switching rate per trial arm as
  the report prints it (Table 2 for design, Figures 4-8 and paras 4.1-4.10 for rates). "Switching" is the report's
  own definition (para 3.15): a loss notification or an internal tariff request, for one or both fuels, inside the trial window, whether or
  not it completed. Nothing is recomputed.
