# RESULT — the pointer vocabulary was layout furniture, and this site points with units of its own record

**Severity:** LATENT · **Lane:** H_harness

`_HERE_RELATIVE` is the here-relative detector every pointer sweep on this site imports. Its noun
list was the furniture a page is drawn with — `table|panel|chart|figure|row|block|section|list|
column|note|box|card|band` — and this site does not point that way. Three live published pointers
were invisible to all four sweeps because of it. All three are repaired in the producer and
republished in this landing. LATENT rather than BLOCKING: no figure moves and no verdict is
invalidated; what was wrong is where three sentences send a reader looking, and the vocabulary
that could not see them.

## The audit was a measurement, not a list of what a page is made of

Every `<noun> above|below` in the published feeds and in the producers' own string literals —
16,083 strings. What the site actually points with is **units of its own record**: a run, an entry,
a finding, a mutation, a refusal, a measurement, a claim. Furniture is the minority, and four of
the thirteen registered nouns (`chart`, `list`, `box`, `card`) match nothing published at all.

Fourteen nouns were added: `run entry item record claim finding mutation refusal check measurement
sentence paragraph summary` (plus the existing thirteen kept).

**Three measured candidates were deliberately REFUSED**, and this is the half worth keeping,
because each reads like an obvious omission to whoever comes next:

| Candidate | The live string that refuses it |
|---|---|
| `reading` | "the highest **reading below** the ceiling is 447" (`simplified.json`) — a threshold |
| `line` | "at least one **line below** zero" (`knowledge_non_commodity_costs.json`) — a cost component going negative |
| `range`/`interval` | "moves the **interval above**" — already refused, with its own measurement, in `tests/tools/test_the_proof_pages_undriven_pointers.py` |

The standing rule this establishes, written into the module beside the pattern: **a site-wide
vocabulary that admits arithmetic starts refusing arithmetic.** A noun earns its place by having no
comparison sense in the live corpus — measured, not assumed. A noun that only points on one page
belongs in that page's own `_EXTRA_PHRASES`, which is the shape the proof rung already chose.

## What the wider vocabulary caught — three live pointers, none reachable before

All three were repaired by **naming the subject**, which takes them out of the vocabulary
altogether. That is the repair the rung's own precedent uses where a referent's homes straddle the
sentence, and it is the stronger one: a named subject is true from anywhere and cannot rot by the
sentence gaining a home.

1. `_legs_on_one_bar:3242` — "The **refusal above** is the one the bounds block itself makes" →
   "This refusal is the bounds block's own". The subject was already named in the same sentence;
   the direction word added nothing and was unchecked.
2. `_population_repair_bias:10622` — "{paths} paths of pricing code away from the **run above**" →
   "away from the run that produced the choosing figure". This is the pointer the item was filed
   for.
3. `_current_world_contrast:10882` — "the **run below** it was taken at {sup}" → "the run this page
   marks superseded was taken at {sup}". This one was **also homeless**: published into
   `why_the_headline_omits_it`, a field no door renders, so its direction was unverifiable by
   construction. Both reds clear on the same repair.

Before the widening the rungs were green on all three. After it, two legs red naming exactly these
— `test_no_tied_here_relative_pointer_is_published_into_a_field_no_door_renders` and
`test_every_tied_here_relative_pointer_is_true_from_the_region_it_lands_in`. After the repairs and
the republish, 17 passed.

## THE RESIDUE, and it is a blind spot the widening exposed rather than closed

There is a **fourth** live pointer and nothing reds on it. `_current_world_contrast`'s
`how_to_read_this` publishes, on its `not is_the_later_run` branch:

> "The same book, the same three arms and the same code as **the figures above**, run at a
> different hour of the same world and from a different commit. It is NOT the more recent of the
> two — **the run above** it was taken at {when} and this one at {mine}…"

**One literal, two directions.** `_here_relative_phrase` returns `_HERE_RELATIVE.search(...)` — the
FIRST match only — so the census registers `figures above` at line 10972 and never sees `run above`
at 10974. The widening made the second phrase matchable and the single-match census still hides it.

This is not speculation: the census was run directly and prints `_current_world_contrast 10972
'figures above'` in the tied half, with no row for 10974.

The site-wide one-home rule is right to leave it alone — it has one home, so the page that owns it
can check it. The gap is in the value_arms rung's census. **The proof rung already solved this
shape**: its `_phrases()` returns a LIST because its sentence carries two directions. The repair is
to give `_here_relative_census` the same treatment — `finditer`, not `search`.

NOT DONE HERE, deliberately: it edits
`tests/tools/test_the_value_arms_pages_undriven_pointers.py`, which is bound to another live claim,
and it is a change to a census that every pointer rung reads. It is a clean next item with its own
exit test — *the census registers every phrase in a literal, not the first* — and the fourth
pointer is the witness that makes it failable.

## What this landing does not claim

That the vocabulary is now complete. It is a detector over prose and says so on its own surface;
`test_the_here_relative_detector_is_aimed_at_prose_this_site_actually_publishes` is what keeps it
honest by requiring it to still be finding live sentences. What is established is narrower and
checkable: the noun list is no longer furniture chosen by intuition, it was audited against the
whole published corpus, and the three nouns kept out are kept out for a stated and reproducible
reason.
