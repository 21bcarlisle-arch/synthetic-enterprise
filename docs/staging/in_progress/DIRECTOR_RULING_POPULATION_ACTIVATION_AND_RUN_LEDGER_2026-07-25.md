## 1. ACTIVATE the generator draw (R13, director's call)

The live seam is still a fixed CUSTOMERS literal — the root cause of the thin book and therefore of every downstream figure the director has rejected (per-customer aggregates, £7 cost-to-serve, sample totals). The reversible half shipped 2026-07-24 (a18821a02). **Activate**, with three conditions:

**Expected and accepted:** downstream re-baselining — fidelity cells, financials, site panels. Historical comparisons that straddle the change are marked, not silently continued. Curriculum values themselves remain director-reserved; this ruling activates the draw, it does not license changing the curriculum.

## 2. Run rotation: stratified, never random

Runs rotate a **stratified grid: world scenario × population seed**. Randomness lives *inside* a cell (which households, which weather path), never in the choice of cell. Coverage of the ratified worlds (history-default, NESO-central, crisis-replay, glut) is guaranteed rather than hoped for, and runs stay comparable. Scenario values remain R13 — this governs sequencing only.

## 3. The run manifest and ledger (every run, one row)

Every run emits: `run_id` · code SHA · curriculum version · world scenario **with its true-probability tag** · population seed **with realised cell counts** · outcomes (survived/died, cause and date if died, EV, £/tCO₂e, worst-cell fidelity). Without this the probability-weighted EV and the unweighted worst-case survival verdicts cannot be computed, and no one can tell whether a change made the company better or merely luckier.

**Artifact retention:** every DEATH retained in full (rare and information-dense); a sample of survivors; anything a fidelity trigger flags. Everything else is the ledger row.

## 4. Three scores, computed from the same runs, never conflated

- **ROBUSTNESS** — unweighted, tail-focused: does the machinery cope with what it is shown? (development signal)
- **COMMERCIAL EV** — probability-weighted by the true measure: what is this company worth?
- **SURVIVAL** — worst-case over the sampled set, unweighted: does it die anywhere?

This restates the ratified importance-sampling discipline (sampling distribution ≠ true probability) and names the three so they cannot be merged by accident. **R15: any aggregate that mixes weighted and unweighted bases must fail loudly.**

## 5. Market segmentation of the P&L

Survival is judged on the **domestic book** — the mission is domestic personalisation-abatement and the 2021–22 evidence base is domestic failure. Any SME/I&C business in the book reports its P&L **separately** and may never silently carry or sink the domestic story. Market-segmented survival criteria are registered as an epoch-2 item, not built now. **First: report what non-domestic business actually exists in the book today** — one line, evidence-based.

## 6. Registered, not built: making survival useful

Binary survival is a weak signal (most runs live; deaths are noisy tail draws). Registered as its own design piece for a director session: **margin of survival** (minimum liquidity headroom and collateral cover reached, recorded from every run including survivors), **breaking strain** (dial world severity until death; record the dose — "survives crisis-replay at 1.0x, dies at 1.4x"), and **cause attribution** on every death and near-death. Survival remains a hard constraint; ranking comes from strain and headroom. Do not build this ahead of the director's session on it.

**Risk & proportionality:** activation re-baselines downstream figures (expected, gated by the coverage report); manifest/ledger are additive; scores are definitional with an R15 guard.
