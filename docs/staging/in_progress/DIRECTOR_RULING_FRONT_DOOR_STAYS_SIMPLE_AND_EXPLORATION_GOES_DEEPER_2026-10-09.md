# [DIRECTOR-RULING → site] — THE FRONT DOOR STAYS SIMPLE; EXPLORATION GOES DEEPER, NOT DENSER (2026-10-09)

**Type:** Director ruling via advisor bridge, from the director's words this morning (2026-10-09,
~05:55 BST), verbatim: *"I don't want the diagram to be full of stats — just a simple visual way to
understand what we are doing, and explore. I like the L2 idea."* Routine site-direction, not a
reserved action. **Severity:** RECORDED · **Lane:** A_strategy_governance


**Knowledge:** none -- a director ruling on the site's front door and exploration, not domain research
---

## What is decided (transmit as decisions)

1. **The front-door diagram stays a simple picture.** A visual, minimal words. No statistics,
   counters, tallies or finding counts join it — ever. The one computed stage word per band
   (Live/Building/Planned, §6 coherence-by-derivation) stays exactly as it is: it is one word and
   it is load-bearing honesty. Nothing numeric gets added beside it. This also formally DECLINES
   the advisor's same-morning suggestion of surfacing per-band open-blocking-finding counts on the
   front door — findings belong on the surfaces that hold findings, not on the door.

2. **Exploration means depth, not density.** Each band of the diagram opens into one L2 sheet — a
   closer look at that band, drawn in the same visual grammar as the door (the wall rendered the
   same way for orientation, the same palette semantics and glyphs, picture first). An L2 sheet
   shows flows and mechanisms a lay reader can follow — e.g. for the world band: regime → weather →
   demand and renewables together → residual → price; for the wall: what crosses in, what crosses
   out, what never crosses. It is not a dashboard and carries no stats either.

3. **One non-negotiable, inherited from §6:** any claim-of-state wording on an L2 sheet (a stage
   word, a built/not-built mark) must DERIVE from the maturity map exactly as the door's stage
   words do — never hand-typed. Hand-synced claims on L2 would reintroduce the defect §6 killed,
   one level down. Narrative mechanism content ("the same weather drives demand up and renewables
   down") is editorial and fine.

## What is yours (open, the seat's call)

- Implementation: in-page expand, linked pages, or whatever fits the site — the director has no
  preference.
- Which bands get sheets first, and timing. In particular, it is reasonable to sequence the
  world-band sheet after the stage-1 world rebuild settles, so its content does not churn weekly.
  No deadline attaches to any of this; it joins the queue at your ranking.
- A July exemplar of the *feel* exists in the advisor's session with the director (a weather→price
  cascade sheet: world cascade on top, the wall as the same black bar with only the relevant
  gates lit, company beliefs below in dashed grammar). The feel is what the director liked; its
  facts are three months stale and bind nothing.

## Risk

- **Touches:** site surface only; no sim/company/saas code.
- **Blast radius:** the front door and new linked/expanded pages, plus the publish gates that read
  them.
- **Probable failure modes:** (a) L2 sheets accrete hand-typed state claims that rot — mitigated by
  the derivation rule in §3 above, which is the condition of the ruling, not a suggestion;
  (b) world-band L2 content churns during the rebuild — mitigated by sequencing, which is yours.
- **Proportionality:** reversible/narrow — just do it, inside the normal weekly publish cadence.
