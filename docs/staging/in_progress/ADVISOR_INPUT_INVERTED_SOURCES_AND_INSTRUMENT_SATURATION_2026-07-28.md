## 1. The candidates — both are the same inversion

The observation that generates both: **every enumerated source runs in one direction.** Simplifications, fidelity ledger, PARTIAL/ABSENT board rows, disqualification battery, claim-status placeholders, sanity findings, Timeframe 2, registered follow-ons — each records *the model does less than reality, and here is the shortfall*. That is the right direction for a fidelity project and the list is good. But a register can only surface what its direction admits.

### A. The overhang register — capability the company lacks by accident, not by design

**The question no existing register asks:** *what could a real UK supplier do that this company is structurally prevented from doing — not by the epistemic wall, which is deliberate and stays, but by an affordance that was simply never built?*

Deliberate hobbling is the wall, the gates, the tiers, R13's curriculum control. Those are the product. **Accidental hobbling is different**: a company that cannot act on something it can legitimately observe, because no seam, no field, or no decision point was ever built for it. A fidelity register does not catch this, because nothing is *wrong* — the model simply never had the option, so no shortfall against reality is ever recorded.

**Why this is not academic here.** The mission is carbon abatement **through personalisation**, measured in £/tCO₂e. If the company cannot personalise along some dimension because of an accidental architectural limit rather than a modelled constraint, **the thesis itself is hobbled and no existing register would show it.** The carbon ledger being designed-and-not-instrumented is already caught by claim-status; this is the class that is not caught by anything.

**Nearest existing things — check these before building anything:** the simplifications register (records choices made, not options never available); the disqualification battery (a practitioner's view of what would make the build not credible — closest relative, and may already cover part of this); `real_world_twin` definitions; the board-spec reconciliations. **If the battery already covers it, say so and this candidate dies** — that is a good outcome.

**Honest cost:** this is the harder register to populate, because absence has no artefact. It probably has to be generated against an external frame (what a real supplier's systems do) rather than read from primary state, which cuts against LAW-C. That tension is real and may be disqualifying. Named, not solved.

### B. The regulatory oracle, inverted — check the SIM, not the company

Canon already holds the principle: **every line of retail energy regulation exists because the failure it addresses actually occurred** — so if the SIM never produces a situation a regulation anticipates, that is a fidelity gap.

As far as the advisor can see from documents, that principle is **stated but never inverted into a running instrument**, and the direction matters:

- The **obligations register** asks *is the company compliant?* — it checks the **company**.
- The **oracle** would ask *does the world ever generate the conditions this rule exists for?* — it checks the **SIM**.

Different subject, different failure caught. A company can be perfectly compliant with a rule whose triggering conditions the world never produces, and both surfaces would read green.

**Why this fits today's ruling particularly well.** The ruling's own diagnosis is that the machine *"has been publishing its own backlog for weeks while treating it as documentation"* — i.e. the enumeration was derived from the project's own opinion of itself. **An oracle anchored in the SLCs cannot do that, because the SLCs do not get a vote.** It is a self-refilling backlog derived from an external corpus. Two routes, one answer: the ruling reached "the registers are the backlog" from the live registers; this reaches "anchor the backlog outside ourselves" from the regulatory principle.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** the advisor is inferring this is unbuilt from the compliance framing in canon and could simply be wrong — `company/regulatory/`, the ComplianceDomain enum and the obligations register may already carry the inverted direction somewhere. **Grep first.** Also: many SLCs anticipate conditions that are genuinely out of scope for this epoch, so the oracle's raw output would need the ratified `deliberate-and-staying` bucket immediately, or it becomes a noise generator. It is a **candidate source, not a mint**, and its first pass should be sized before it is trusted.

### C. The level ladder is an instrument, and instruments saturate

Today's ruling holds that saturation was **a bookkeeping artifact, not a fact about the world**. That is correct as diagnosis and this note does not contest it.

The addition is narrower: **a level ladder with fixed targets is an eval, and evals saturate by construction.** Practice in this space is that a saturated eval is *replaced*, not patched — it is thrown away and a new one built, because a maxed instrument stops discriminating. The gap registers are a genuine and large refill, but they **postpone** saturation rather than preventing it: they extend the ladder's supply of work, they do not change what the ladder measures.

**Where this points:** survival and £/tCO₂e do not saturate the way a level ladder does. Mortality and the longevity tournament have been framed as the next epoch's *content*; this suggests they are also **the next instrument** — the thing that keeps discriminating after L-levels stop. That is a resequencing argument, offered as an argument, not a recommendation. It is the director's call and the agent's evidence.

### D. Dynamic workflows are epoch-4's execution substrate, not a tooling upgrade

The parallelism correction now in `ADVISOR_ADDENDUM_HARNESS_PRUNING_TRIGGER_FIRED_2026-07-28` §2.8 separates lane parallelism (N writers, N merge homes — expensive, dangerous to widen) from task-decomposition parallelism (one writer, wide sandboxed interior, only results come home).

The consequence has not been drawn anywhere: **the evolutionary tournament is exactly the second shape.** N independent lives, no shared tree, no merges, only results returning. It is the one thing in this project that genuinely wants very wide fan-out — and it has been parked two epochs out while the fan-out argument has been conducted entirely in the lane where width is expensive and dangerous.

Nearer-term instance of the same shape: the **spike-tail search** (model max £574 vs real £4,038, standing as the highest-priority fidelity defect) is a parameter search over an existing scorer — G1's CVaR worst-cell machinery is already built. Also no shared tree, also nothing to merge.

**Both remain gated on the token-headroom sensor**, not on safety — see the addendum's item 13. Sensor first, then pilot.
