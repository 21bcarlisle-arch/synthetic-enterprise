**Type:** [DECISION ×2]. Ratified in conversation with the director 2026-08-05, machine offline; lands with the Saturday batch. ## Ruling 1 — The H territory becomes two lanes, after distribution

**Distribute first.** Atoms whose SUBJECT belongs to another lane emigrate to it, regardless of what machinery they use: the payment belief-gap atom to D (it names itself part of the D5 decomposition), the fabric belief-gap atom to W1, control mutation-tests to the lanes owning the controls, judge validation to the lane owning each judged organ. Principle: verification-of-X is part of X's maturity — a method is not a lane. Audit independence is preserved where it actually lives — in execution context (amnesiac organs, cold-eyes, restricted context) — and becomes a standing clause in each receiving lane's charter, not a wall on the map.

**Then two residual lanes:**
- **H — The Build Engine.** How work is granted and isolated: draw, supervisor, worktrees, git locking, gate hygiene, H's own exit criterion. (~14 atoms.)
- **OPS — Running the Machine.** Daemon lifecycle, launcher cutover, housekeeping, publish pipeline, stall handling, escalation to the director — plus the posture shelf (security profiles, DR, NFR registers): running the machine safely is operations. (~14 atoms.)

Satellite prefixes (HX, GAP, H_GAP, OPS1, bare H_) dissolve into proper ids inside these two. Both lanes are past the map's own dial-3 threshold and owe charters — **CC drafts and adopts them; charters take effect on adoption.** **The mint gate gains a number registry:** duplicate numbers exist today (two H23s, two H24s, two H27s) and must be impossible after this. Exact per-atom destinations are **CC's to decide and file** — the cut is ruled, the filing is maintenance.

## Ruling 2 — Plain-words naming law (map-wide, lanes and atoms)

The director's words: letters and atom numbers do not communicate the architecture; he cannot judge whether groupings are logical without words or pictures. Therefore:

- **Every lane and every atom carries a plain-English title** (a few words, subject not mechanism) **and a one-line gain statement** — what the company or machine gains when this is done. Mechanism vocabulary (draw, worktree, mutation, marker, hook) lives in description fields, never titles.
- **Ids become machine keys.** Grammar-enforced, registry-checked at mint, and never shown on any human surface — chat, site, reports, NTFYs all render titles.
- **The map's site view renders titles**, lanes as named shelves, level as the visual dial; ids are debug-hover at most. The didactic rule already governing site graphs applies to the map picture.
- **Acceptance test (falsifiable, retrospective):** the director, reading a lane's atom titles cold on his phone, can spot a misfiled atom. A misfiling invisible in titles is a naming failure. Run at each epoch close alongside consolidation.
- Field mechanics (whether `name` is repurposed or a `title` field added) are CC's choice; the law is the readable surface, not the schema.

## Operating mode — see-and-correct, not approve

Director ruling, general in scope: **he is out of the loop by default, in it only by exception.** Nothing in this ruling waits on him. Filings, renames, charter adoption, satellite dissolution — all proceed; all are visible (map diff, LATEST, NTFY); he course-corrects retrospectively, and reversal is cheap because every move here is metadata. Governance-weight moves — creating or merging a lane — proceed the same way but are flagged prominently at the time, so retrospective correction has something to catch. The only standing gates anywhere in the system remain the true doors: irreversible or external acts (substrate swaps, spend, outside contact). Reversible governance is never a door.
