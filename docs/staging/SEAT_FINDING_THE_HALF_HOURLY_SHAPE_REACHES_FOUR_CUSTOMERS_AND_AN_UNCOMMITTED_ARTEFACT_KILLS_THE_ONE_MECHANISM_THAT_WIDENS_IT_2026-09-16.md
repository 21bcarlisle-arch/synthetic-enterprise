## Part 2 — and the mechanism that widens coverage is dead on this tree

`simulation/weather_cell_siting.cell_matched_site` exists for precisely this. Its docstring:
`weather_inputs._weather_source_customer_id` resolves a premise to an archive by **exact `location`
dict identity** — two customers share a CSV only if they share a coordinate to the fourth decimal —
and that refusal *"is honest and it is also blind: it cannot say whether the premise's weather is
genuinely unlike the four sites, or merely unlike them in the fourth decimal of a latitude."* The
derived cells can say. It is wired: `simulation/weather_inputs.py:58` calls it.

**It currently accepts nothing.** Measured, one variable, without touching another lane's working
copy — `cell_matched_site` takes its artefact path as an argument:

| location | working tree | HEAD's artefact |
|---|---|---|
| Cornish coast (50.4689, −4.1492) | `None` | **`C1`** |
| midlands (53.0, −2.0) | `None` | `None` |
| edinburgh (55.9, −3.2) | `None` | `None` |

**At HEAD the accept branch fires. On this working tree it fires for nothing.** The cause is
`sim/weather_cells/site_cells.json` and `sim/weather_cells/occupied_land_cells.csv`, both **modified
and uncommitted** — regenerated by another lane, and the regenerated partition matches no site.

## What this costs, and why it is BLOCKING rather than LATENT

`tests/simulation/test_weather_cell_siting.py` is red on two legs and **both are correct**:

- `test_the_accept_branch_is_reachable_and_it_matches_climate_not_proximity` — `assert None == 'C1'`
- `test_one_driver_disagreeing_refuses_the_whole_substitution` — `assert 5 == 10`

The first is the exact control CLAUDE.md prescribes for this situation: *"when a branch exists to be
taken rarely, assert it CAN be taken before asserting what it does"*. It is doing its job.

If that artefact lands, the only mechanism that can extend the fabric shape beyond four exact
coordinates is switched off, silently — every premise falls back to `legacy_pc1_rescaled`, every
coverage refusal stays honest, and no figure anywhere changes to say so. **The 1.9% would become a
ceiling rather than a floor.** It is uncommitted, so it has not reached the book; it is in the shared
tree, so it reaches the book on the next landing that sweeps it.

## What I did NOT do

**I did not touch either artefact, or the module.** They are another lane's uncommitted work. The
comparison above was made by passing HEAD's artefact as an argument, in-process, which changes
nothing on disk. Whoever owns that regeneration owns the fix, and this document is how they find out
rather than discovering it in a red gate.

I also did not re-derive the partition to see whether the regeneration is *wrong* or merely
*different*. `--derive` writes both artefacts from one run, so they cannot be regenerated apart, and
re-running it would overwrite their work. **A regeneration that matches no site is either a real
defect or a partition that moved for a good reason nobody has written down** — and I cannot tell
which from outside their lane.

## What the third Stage 1 item actually needs

Named here so it is not re-derived:

1. **The artefact regression resolved**, by its lane, before anything else — the widening mechanism
   has to be alive before widening it means anything.
2. **More weather archives.** Four sites is the binding constraint on 213 of 228 customers. The
   module already measured that the four occupy 4 of the 21 cells on each driver; the household
   share of GB they cover is in its own docstring. Pulling Open-Meteo for the cells the book
   actually occupies is the direct route, and Open-Meteo is already a named source.
3. **The eligibility verdict should name the SITE it could not find**, not just the reason. The
   artefact records `customer_id`, `is_eligible`, `reason` — so 213 identical refusals cannot tell
   anyone *which* archives to pull. A refusal that names its class but not its subject cannot be
   acted on without re-deriving the whole thing, which is what I just had to do.
