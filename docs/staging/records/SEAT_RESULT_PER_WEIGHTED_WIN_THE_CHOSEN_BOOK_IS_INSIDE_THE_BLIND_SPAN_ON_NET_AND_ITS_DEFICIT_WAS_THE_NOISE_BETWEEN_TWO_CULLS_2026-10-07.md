# Per weighted win, the chosen book is inside the blind span on net, and its deficit was the noise between two culls

**Verdict.** Compare like with like, per settlement-weighted commercial win, and against the
count-matched cull (`cull52`). The chosen book (ARM B) is then **£14.6 per win above ARM C on
net**, about 0.1 standard errors. Its gross margin per win is £27.7 below. Across the blind span
B is **inside on net** and below every blind book on gross by 1.1%, which is less than a tenth
of one standard error. The −£174 per win it trailed `cull57` by was not a property of B. Two
uniform culls of the same 497 wins, 52 and 57 accounts, **differ from each other by £189 per
win on net**. B's gap against C57 was smaller than the gap between the two baselines.

**Correction beside the 2026-10-07 result's §4** ("Weighted, the net sign survives"). It does not
survive against the count-matched arm. That reading's cause was C57's own sampling error, not
B's.

Fast mode, one seed, one world. The pounds are diagnostics, not publishable figures. Whether the
sign is real across books stays with the director's EP17 row.

settlement weights and divided by the wins they stand for (497; 495.6 for A's cull). The ± is
the linearised sampling error for a fixed total weight. B's Kish effective sample is **28.1 of
52 accounts**, so its error is the largest.

## 3. B's −£230 gross margin per win against C57, decomposed

The decomposition is exact: gross per win = settled months per win × kWh per account-month ×
gross per kWh, by fuel. The legs are Shapley-averaged over the three factors, so the order of
substitution does not matter. Remainder £0.00.

| leg | B − C57 | B − cull52 |
|---|---:|---:|
| tenure (settled months per weighted win: B 34.37, C57 41.44, cull52 36.10) | **−502.5** | −126.6 |
| volume (kWh per account-month: elec B 328.5 / C57 285.5 / cull52 307.1) | **+407.8** | +236.2 |
| margin rate (gross per kWh: elec B 0.1559 / C57 0.1677 / cull52 0.1710) | −135.4 | −137.3 |
| **total** | **−230.1** | **−27.7** |

**Weight-fit error is not the cause.** The weights are fitted on the ex-ante axes, and on those
axes B is the arm closest to all 497 wins:

| weighted mean | all 497 wins | B | C57 | cull52 | A cull | D tenure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| floor area m² | 86.51 | **86.22** | 91.75 | 91.73 | 92.52 | 90.14 |
| fabric W/K | 177.67 | **178.13** | 177.90 | 176.09 | 174.79 | 203.87 |
| planned customer-years | 4.764 | 4.830 | 4.675 | 4.669 | 4.69 | 4.76 |

All three legs sit in **realised** outcomes, not in anything the weights could see:

- **Tenure.** B's weighted wins stay 59.3% of their planned months; C57's stay 73.9%. Five
  accounts carry 28% of B's weight. Three of them left early: PROS-2019-0012 after 13 of 83
  planned months, PROS-2020-0177 after 13 of 67, and PROS-2018-0260 after 35 of 88. Without the
  top five, B's months per win go from 34.4 to 37.7. A model-assisted reading puts the
  population at 37.3: realised months regressed on the ex-ante axes over all 220 accounts
  settled in any arm, R² 0.36, then predicted for all 497 wins. So B is 2.9 months below that
  estimate, C57 is 4.2 above it, and cull52 is 1.2 below.
- **Volume.** B's weighted kWh per account-month is 1,215 against a model-assisted 1,058
  (R² 0.50). C57's is 1,028 and cull52's is 1,069. That is the same shape: a few heavy weights
  on high-demand homes.
- **Margin rate.** Gross per kWh falls as volume rises, because fixed charges are spread over
  more kWh. So part of this leg is the volume leg seen through the tariff. **That remainder is
  named, not attributed**: separating the standing-charge share from tariff-year mix needs
  per-account standing and unit revenue, which the arm output does not carry.

**Book-level coupling is negligible.** The accounts settled in both B and C57 (8) differ by
−0.09% in gross. Those in both B and cull52 (7) differ by −0.15%. An account's P&L does not
depend on which others are settled, so the arms really are estimators of one quantity.

**Attribution, in one line.** B's −230 against C57 is realised-outcome sampling error at a
Kish n of 28 against 57: tenure −502 and volume +408 offset each other, and the rate leg −135
is partly volume. Against the count-matched cull the same legs net to −28, and the deficit is
gone.
