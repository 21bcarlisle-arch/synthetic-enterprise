## The design's own hypothesis is refuted on one row, and that inverts how its verdict reads

Common random numbers only cancel while the two legs' books stay together, and the size term
changes prices, which changes who stays — so the tool published `pairing_bought` to find out
rather than assume. On **enterprise value** the paired spread came out **wider** than two
independent draws would have been (1.19×). Its "cannot tell" is therefore **the best available
ruler failing to resolve the move, not a worse ruler being used** — the opposite of a caveat, and
the surface states it rather than leaving the ratio column to be interpreted. On the other three
the pairing bought something, most of all on gross margin (0.51×).

At five pairs this was true of net margin as well (1.11×) and it went away with the sixth. The
five-pair reading is kept here rather than quietly replaced: a ratio that flips on one seed is
itself a fact about how little this book resolves.
