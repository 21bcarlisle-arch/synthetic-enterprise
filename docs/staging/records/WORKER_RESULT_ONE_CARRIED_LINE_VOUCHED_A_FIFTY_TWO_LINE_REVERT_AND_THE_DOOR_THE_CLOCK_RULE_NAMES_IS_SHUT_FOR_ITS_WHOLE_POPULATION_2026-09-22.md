## 3. The publish path never went through the door at all

The refusal guards a COMMIT. `generate_dashboard_json` imports the **working-tree** copy of
`tools/generate_*.py`, runs it, and writes `site/data/*.json` whose mtime is the moment of writing —
so `clock_judge` exempts the output by construction and nothing could tell a feed regenerated from

Closed **per producer, at the import**, not by a pre-flight over the whole cycle: a whole-cycle
refusal would wedge publishing on the normal resting state of a shared checkout, which is the
pressure toward bypass the module exists to remove. The stale module is replaced in `sys.modules`

## 4. THE FINDING THIS LEAVES OPEN: the remedy names a door that is shut for its entire population

`Loss.remedy()` tells a clock-stale path to run `python3 -m tools.refresh_to_head <path>`. Run on
the eight clock-stale documents left on this tree, **all eight are refused**:

| verdict | paths |
|---|---|
| `refused_no_reader` | 6 — every `.md` and `.yaml` |
| `refused_supplies_names_head_lacks` | 2 — the `.json` pair, a real content judgement |

`judge_copy` refuses any suffix outside `READABLE + DATA_SUFFIXES`, and `.md`/`.yaml` are outside
both. So the clock rule — added 2026-09-22 precisely because *"the maturity map, the knowledge
layer, the simplification notes and the staging record"* had no door — names a remedy that
structurally cannot act on the maturity map, the knowledge layer, the simplification notes or the
staging record. **The rule I added this turn inherits it**: `docs/data-sources/weather.md` gets the
same shut door.

This is the shape `Loss.render(merge=True)` already exists to avoid — *printing a door the tool
would refuse* — arriving through the other rule.

**The repair is not a widening of `judge_copy`'s reader**, and that is the point worth carrying: the
clock has already established what the symbol reader exists to establish. What it has NOT
established is *"the copy has nothing to lose"* — a clock-stale copy can carry the holder's own
edits made before the landing, and `NOTE_STALE` in the fixtures is exactly that shape. So the honest
move is a NAMED verdict state for it, writable only behind an explicit flag, on the same reasoning
as `--superseded`: the preserved ref makes the loss recoverable and named, and the choice to accept
it is a judgement, not a reading. A refusal that says which is worth more than a verdict that picks
the flattering side.
