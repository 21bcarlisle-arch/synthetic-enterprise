# PRE-REGISTRATION — the unbounded-quotient census, by shape

**Filed:** 2026-09-22, before any candidate site was read. Drawn as Lane 0 delivery,
`the-unbounded-quotient-is-caught-as-a-class-by-a-control-not-as-a-fifth-instance-by-hand`.

## The rule being censused

A published count derived as a quotient `(bar / estimate)^2` — or any quotient whose
DENOMINATOR is an estimate that the same artefact grades against its own bar — has **no upper
bound** in exactly the state where a reader wants it. Clearing the bar IS the statement that the
denominator's interval excludes zero; failing it IS the statement that the interval contains zero;
and a denominator that may be zero prices the question at no finite number. So the count must be
gated on that grade, published `None` with a named reason otherwise, and the arithmetic kept under
a name that is not a plan (`*_at_the_point_estimate`, never `*_needed`).

Four instances were found and fixed ONE AT A TIME — `06e316ae4`, `5742edb1c` (two page keys),
`964036259` (the money leg's producer), `949f80894` (the rank leg's). Each was found by a human-ish
read of a neighbouring block. **Nothing in the tree can see this shape.**

## What I am about to measure

A census across `tools/`, `saas/` and `company/` **by the SHAPE and not by the name**. The four
found so far all spell it `*_needed_to_state_a_sign`; the census asks instead for *any* published
quotient whose denominator is a graded estimate.

## The predictions, written before the candidate sites were read

**P1 — a fifth instance exists under a different name.** Four hand-found instances sharing one
naming convention is evidence the convention, not the rule, is what was searched. I predict the
by-shape census finds **at least one** site outside the `*_needed_to_state_a_sign` family that
publishes a count whose denominator is a graded estimate, ungated.

**P2 — the control, not the count, is the deliverable.** I predict that whatever the census finds,
the durable output is a control over the CLASS that would have red-flagged all four landed
instances before their fixes. A control that only names today's five sites is the instance fix
again, wearing a control's clothes.

**P3 — the commonest near-miss is a fixed TOLERANCE in the denominator.** `n = (z/tol)^2 * var`
is the ordinary sample-size formula and is NOT this defect: its denominator is a chosen tolerance,
not an estimate the artefact grades. I predict the census turns up such sites and that
distinguishing them is the hard half of writing the control — a control that reds on them is a
control that will be deleted.

**P4 — I do not know whether the fifth instance is in `saas/` or `company/` at all.** Both may be
clean; the shape may live only in `tools/`. If so, that is a result and not a failure, and the
control still earns its place because the shape can arrive tomorrow.

## What refutes each

P1 is refuted by a complete by-shape census turning up no ungated site outside the naming family.
P3 is refuted by there being no fixed-tolerance sample-size site in the tree. P2 cannot be refuted
by measurement — it is a design commitment, recorded here so that a control keyed to today's five
sites can be read afterwards as the failure it would be.
