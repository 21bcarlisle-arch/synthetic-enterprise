# PRE-REGISTRATION — acquisition selects on each prospect's own responsiveness (2026-10-08)

**Ruled by the director, 2026-10-08:** *"Acquisition selects on each prospect's own responsiveness:
approved, as your option 1, with option 2 run both ways and reported, using the pre-registered test
you set out. No past-switching carry-over term."* Filed BEFORE any run of the changed world.

## The change

- **Option 1 (on in both arms).** The acquisition funnel's price stage reads each prospect's own
  price sensitivity, as the renewal roll already does, instead of one population response. Prospects
  enter the market weighted by their own chance of shopping. No new number; the latent value never
  crosses the seam.
- **Option 2, run both ways.** Arm I draws the high/medium/low sensitivity level independently of
  engagement, as today. Arm L draws it by rank on engagement. Both keep the 2% segment R² and the 1.26×
  spread. Both are reported, and no middle is chosen.
- **Not built:** any past-switching carry-over term.

## The test (B8's coin-drawn decision set, fresh seeds 101 and 202)

The company is given a price-legal read by acquisition route, time on the default tariff, or
ever-actively-renewed. These are the observables a save offer may now vary by (director, 2026-10-08).
Option 1 "matters" only if ALL hold, on both seeds:

1. **P1.** At least one observable group reaches break-even on a save offer.
2. **P2.** The learned decision offers to that group only.
3. **P3.** It beats both flat rules (never offer, always offer) by more than two seed-noise standard
   deviations.
4. **P4, the control.** With option 1 OFF, the same decision falls back to offering nothing. If it
   does not, any lift came from correlates the world already carried, not from selection.

## Predictions, written before the runs

- **A won book is more responsive than the market.** Its mean latent price sensitivity at win is
  above the market draw's, by roughly the ratio of the shopping rates of winners to the population.
  Prediction: winners' sensitivity is 1.1-1.4× the market mean in arm I, and higher in arm L.
- **P1-P3 hold in arm L on at least one seed, and fail in arm I on both.** In arm I the selection
  runs only through shopping, so I expect it to be too weak at a 2% sensitivity share.
- **P4 holds in both arms.**

A refutation of any of these is recorded beside this file; nothing here is revised after the runs.
