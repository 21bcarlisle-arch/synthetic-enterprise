# PRE-REGISTRATION — which live surfaces still assert selection without the nine-seed band

Filed **before** reading `explore/`, `knowledge/`, `capabilities_door.json`, `dd_opening_arms.json`,
`book_growth.json`, `delivery.json` and `proof.json`. What is already read at filing time, and is
therefore **not** predicted here, is stated as such.

## The population, established before the predictions

A surface is **live** if a page a reader can open fetches it or hand-authors it. Measured by
`grep -o 'data/[a-z_0-9]*\.json'` over the five `index.html` files:

| Page | Feeds it fetches |
|---|---|
| `site/index.html` | `dashboard`, `explore_carbon`, `moap_node_atoms` + hand-authored hero/thesis prose |
| `site/capabilities/index.html` | `book_growth`, `capabilities_door`, `dd_opening_arms`, `value_arms` |
| `site/explore/index.html` | `customers`, `explore_carbon`, `explore_hh_days`, `weather` |
| `site/harness/index.html` | `delivery`, `director_delta`, `director_reserved`, `proof` |
| `site/knowledge/index.html` | `knowledge_review`, `knowledge_wholesale` |

**Already read, not predicted:** `value_arms.json` carries the band and the withheld verdict in
`headline`, `current_world.selection_leg` and `contrast_bounds`; `capabilities/index.html` renders
them from the feed rather than authoring them. `site/index.html`'s `.thesis` block hand-authors
*"you create value fastest by knowing each household well"* and names no band.

## The predictions

**P1.** Of the 60 files in `site/data/`, **fewer than half** are reached by any of the five pages.
In particular `simplified.json` (130 selection-word hits) and `company.json`, `capabilities.json`,
`world.json`, `method.json` reach **no reader at all** — so their selection language is not a live
claim and the sweep must say *unrendered*, not *clean*.

**P2.** `explore/`, `knowledge/`, `book_growth.json`, `capabilities_door.json`,
`dd_opening_arms.json`, `weather.json`, `explore_hh_days.json` and `customers.json` assert
**nothing** about where the advantage comes from. They are household/market surfaces, not thesis
surfaces. Expected verdict: NO CLAIM.

**P3.** `delivery.json` and `proof.json` — reached by `harness/` — **do** carry the direction, and
carry it **honestly**, because they were regenerated after the floor landed. Specifically
`delivery.json.what_it_decided.focus[0].why` already reads "£319 is the choosing, inside the noise".
Expected verdict: ALREADY HONEST. **The risk to this prediction is that the honest sentence is in a
field no renderer reads** — that is the [[a-pointers-referent-can-render-nowhere]] shape, and it is
checked at both ends, not assumed from the field's presence.

**P4 — the one I expect to have to fix.** `site/index.html`'s `.thesis` block is the **only** live
surface asserting selection as the source of value without the band. Its `NOT YET MEASURED` tag is
attached to the **carbon** score (£/tCO₂e), not to the money leg, so a reader meets the
personalisation claim with no signal that the money side has been measured and returned
*cannot resolve*.

**What would refute P4:** any second live surface making the directional claim. If one is found,
P4 is refuted as *"the only"* and the finding says so beside this prediction rather than above it.

## What this pre-registration cannot decide

Whether the front-door thesis sentence is *wrong*. It is a **claim about what the company is
testing**, and a company may state its hypothesis. What it may not do is state it where the reader
cannot tell that the hypothesis has been put to a run and the run declined to name a direction. The
remedy is therefore an addition, never a deletion — [[when-a-surface-has-no-floor-say-so-on-the-surface]].
