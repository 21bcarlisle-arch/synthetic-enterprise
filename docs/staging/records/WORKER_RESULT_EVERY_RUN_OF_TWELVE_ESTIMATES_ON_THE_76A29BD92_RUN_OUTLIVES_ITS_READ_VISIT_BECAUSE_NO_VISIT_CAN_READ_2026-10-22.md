`billing_basis == "estimated"` bills. 12 or more counts. Meter type is from `meter_read_log`. A visit
is a bill in the run, or the bill that ended it, with `read_instruction_visit_sent` True. "Ended by"

are summed over the run's estimated bills (`total_consumption_kwh`, `true_consumption_kwh`). The
