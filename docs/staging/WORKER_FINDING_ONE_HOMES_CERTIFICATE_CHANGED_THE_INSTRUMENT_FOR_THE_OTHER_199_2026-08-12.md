## 1. The defect, reproduced: an epsilon in one certificate, a different instrument for everyone

`panel_mirror` reflects the truth through the register. `_reflect_level` (same ABSOLUTE error,
opposite sign) is the default; it is undefined where `2*epc <= actual`, and there the WHOLE
panel falls back to `_reflect` (same RATIO error). `_level_reflection_is_feasible` is a
threshold, so the instrument is a **step function of the worst certificate in the panel**.

Measured on this atom's own drawn 200, walking its worst home across the boundary and moving
nothing else — a **two-parts-per-million** change in one home's register:

| ratio `actual/epc` on that one home | reflection | premises breaching the level promise | worst breach | register MAE (kW/K) | mirror money, GBP/premise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.999999 | level_preserving | 0 of 200 | 1.79e-15 | 0.044774 | +296 |
| 2.000001 | log_preserving_fallback | **175 of 200** | **4.26** (426% of a home's own error) | 0.074085 | **+341** |

None of the other 199 homes moved. Holding the population fixed and switching only the
instrument, on the two PUBLISHED populations:

* drawn 200 — mirror money **+296 → +341** per premise (**+15.4%**), register MAE 0.0448 →
