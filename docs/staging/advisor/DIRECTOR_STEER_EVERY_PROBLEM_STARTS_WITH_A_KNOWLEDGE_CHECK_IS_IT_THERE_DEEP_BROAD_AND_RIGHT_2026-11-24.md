# [DIRECTOR-STEER] Every problem starts with a knowledge check: is the knowledge there, is it deep and broad enough, is it right?

**Type:** STEER, through the advisor channel. The director's words, from chat, Tue 24 Nov 05:56 GMT, after the price chain was found built to L4 with no wholesale price-formation page beneath it:

- "It should be a check when we get a problem, is the knowledge there, is it deep and broad enough? is it right?"

## The intent

When something goes wrong (a finding, a gate breach, a cold-read fault, a model error, a prediction miss), the **first question is about knowledge, before code.** There are four parts:

1. **Is the knowledge there?** Is there a page covering this area at all?
2. **Is it deep enough?** Does it explain the mechanism (what causes it, what moves it), not just a number?
3. **Is it broad enough?** Does it cover the drivers a practitioner would name first? The price chain missed gas drivers, carbon, interconnectors and CfDs.
4. **Is it right?** Does it pass the 22 Nov "second view": a second source, a per-unit restatement, an order-of-magnitude check, an identity? Has a practitioner read it?

If any answer is no, **the knowledge work comes before the fix.** The fix then cites the page.

## How (the machine owns the design, and it must not be a new rulebook)

- **Build it into the one work system's item shape.** The 14 Nov steer already asks, at intake, "whether a knowledge gap sits behind it". Make that four fields: **present / deep / broad / right**. Each is answered with the page it rests on, or with "no page". An item whose four fields are not filled is not ready to draw. An item answered "no" spawns its knowledge item ahead of itself in the same lane.
- **Make it mechanical where it can be.** The draw refuses an item with an empty knowledge check, like the recommendation guard refuses a bare ask. Keep the check small: four short answers, not an essay.
- **Run it backwards once:** the knowledge check over the open items and over every world and supplier model at L3 or above, starting with the world models, which K8 has not been enforced on (the 05:40 finding). List those with no page or a thin one. Rank them by how much each feeds a published figure.
- **Learning-loop measure:** "problems whose root cause was missing, shallow, narrow or wrong knowledge". It is expected to be most of them early on. Watch whether it falls as the pages deepen. Beside it, keep the 22 Nov measure: nonsense found by the director first.

Place it in Sunday's review under harness, as part of the one work system's next increment, not as a separate mechanism.
