# [DIRECTOR-STEER] Record where research is absent, and search beyond the UK (2026-11-11)

**Type:** STEER, through the advisor channel. It adds to `DIRECTOR_STEER_GAP_DRIVEN_LITERATURE_SEARCH_AT_TWO_DEPTHS_2026-11-11.md`. The director's words, from chat on Wed 11 Nov:

## 1. An absence of research is a finding

When a gap's search finds little or nothing, record that as a result, not as a failed search. Say:
- what was searched;
- where;
- what came closest;
- why that falls short: wrong country, wrong period, wrong population, a model rather than evidence, or nothing at all.

It matters three ways:
- It confirms a GAP is a real gap, so the code carries it as a ranged `None` honestly (K2).
- It marks where the practitioner's knowledge (K4) is the only source, so the question goes to him with a recommendation.
- It shows where Poesys could add something no one else has, which matters given M4's "the method is the asset".

Keep a short, visible list of these "no published evidence" areas on the Knowledge tab, each linked to its gap.

## 2. Search beyond the UK

The 08:10 steer said "UK sources first". That stays true as a *seed*, not a boundary. Physics does not change at the border, and much of household behaviour carries across too. Search internationally, and give every non-UK source a **transferability** note:
- **Physics** (solar yield, heat loss, battery and EV behaviour): transfers fully once climate and housing stock are adjusted for.
- **Behaviour** (adoption, response to price, effort tolerance): transfers partly. Say what differs: tariffs and market design, regulation, climate, housing, income, culture.
- **Rules, prices and market structure:** UK-specific. Foreign evidence is an analogue only.

Grade evidence and transferability separately. A strong trial from another country can be weak evidence for GB. The existing "Other markets" reference area holds what is market-specific. What transfers belongs on the topic's own page, with its note.

This also prepares for extending beyond the UK later, which is his call when it comes. Nothing is built for that now.

## Risk

Touches: the reading log, knowledge pages and the Knowledge tab.
Blast radius: knowledge only.
Probable failure mode: foreign figures entering a GB constant without adjustment. A non-UK source never sets a GB value without its transferability note and the adjustment stated.
Proportionality: reversible and narrow — just do it.
