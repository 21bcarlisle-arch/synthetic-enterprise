Drawn as `the-supervisors-publish-gate-read-revert-is-a-second-subject-its-author-must-land`.
The pre-registration and result for the work that came OUT of it:
`docs/staging/records/PREREG_THE_WEDGE_READER_NEVER_TRIMS_THE_WINDOW_ITS_MESSAGE_CLAIMS_2026-09-24.md`.

## The item's premise, re-measured — and it was spent in the direction that costs most

The item asked for one thing: land `background/supervisor.py`'s uncommitted "revert of
`shared_tree_live_record(...)` on `_publish_gate_wedge_active`'s two reads", which it called *a
second subject its author must land*. The draw's own path classifier tagged the coupled test file
`holder work` — *"supplies 6 names HEAD lacks"* — and named `surgical_land --content` as the door.

**Every one of those readings is an artefact of the base.** The classifier asks the SHARED TREE's
HEAD, which was 16 commits behind `origin/main`. Measured against the trunk instead:

| path | vs `origin/main` | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `background/supervisor.py` | +17 working-only lines, −30 trunk-only | **stale** — all 17 are older revisions of comments and the two reads |
| `background/delivery_lane.py` | **+0** working-only lines, −61 | **pure stale checkout** — supplies nothing |
| `site/test_the_book_is_bounded_by_compute_reaches_the_reader.py` | identical | nothing to land |
| `tests/background/test_harden_rung_pass_ceiling.py` | identical | nothing to land |
| `tests/tools/test_discovery_pass_ceiling.py` | identical | nothing to land |
| `tests/background/test_publish_gate_wedge_draw.py` | +98 / −161 | **genuine fork** — 5 names the trunk lacks, 6 it would delete |

So the item's literal instruction — land the supervisor revert — would have **reverted
`scoped_gate_unjudged` off the trunk**: the sixth-cause instrument, whose absence is the subject of
two other live findings in this same queue. The working copy is dated 2026-09-17 and predates it.
The item's other instruction, a 3-way merge for `delivery_lane.py` because *"trunk supplies lines it
lacks"*, is spent in the arithmetic sense: the working copy supplies **zero** lines, so
`refresh_to_head` is lossless and a merge is not needed.

This is the shape already on the map — *an item's prescribed `--content` door can revert the very
symbol the item names as its goal* — but with a sharper edge on it: here the door was named by the
**classifier**, not by the item's author, and the classifier printed its own base caveat correctly.
The caveat and the verdict were both in the draw, and only the verdict is phrased as an instruction.

## What was actually there, and it was worth the turn

The one genuine fork was the test file, and the five controls it holds are not a rewrite of the six
on the trunk — they are the **control half of a production change that was never written**.
`supervisor.PUBLISH_GATE_WINDOW_SECONDS` did not exist; the reader never trimmed the window its own
message claims. Union-merged, both halves are now green together: the trim landed with the six
kind-reader controls intact, which landing the shared copy wholesale would have deleted.

## What this costs if nothing changes

`holder work` is the ONE tag the draw licenses `isolate_hunks --survey` + `surgical_land --content`
for. On a behind base that tag is not conservative — it is **actively inverted**: the staler the
working copy, the more names it appears to "supply that HEAD lacks", because HEAD is the thing that
moved. A lane that trusts the tag and skips the caveat lands old bytes over new ones through the
sanctioned door, and the commit message says it landed holder work.

The cheap remedy is not another register: it is for the classifier to refuse to emit a per-path
remedy at all while its base is behind, rather than emitting one and appending a caveat. It already
computes the ancestry — it prints the "16 commit(s) behind" line from it. A verdict it declines to
give cannot be followed.
