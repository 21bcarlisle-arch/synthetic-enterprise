## What the drawn direction asked, and what closes it

Thirteen here-relative producer literals were **recorded and not judged** by the two landed sweeps:
twelve in `tools/generate_value_arms_data.py`, one in `tools/generate_proof_data.py`. The
published-feed sweep cannot see a sentence that never reaches the feed; the producer sweep's rule 3
can only refuse an untied literal when that producer's feed already carries a multi-home field, and
`value_arms.json` carries none. **Only running the producer's branches closes them.**

`tests/tools/test_the_value_arms_pages_undriven_pointers.py` does that for the twelve. It drives
each undriven branch through `build()` over the **six real artefacts** `generate()` reads, reads the
sentence's landing fields out of that build by marker, and derives its homes by driving the real
`/capabilities/` door down its own boot path against that build. **The thing each pointer points at
is probed the same way**, so direction is judged region-against-region with both sides measured and
neither written down.

**The one in `generate_proof_data.py` is not closed by this and is stated as open**, below.

### The false one, and it was false twice over

`_departures` told a reader the accounts the arm's own price drove out "**ARE named above**". Driven,
that sentence renders in `#arms-decisions`. The field it points at,
`priced_accounts_the_arm_itself_drove_out`, renders in `#arms-decisions` too — so "above" was the
wrong direction — and **no region above it names an account at all**.

The first repair was to "beside this", on the reading that the two share a region. **The rung caught
that too**, and that is the part worth keeping: marker-probed against the real build *and* the
published one, `priced_accounts_the_arm_itself_drove_out` renders in **no region of any door**. There
was nothing at the end of the pointer in any direction. Recorded here beside the wrong first repair
rather than revised away.

The landed wording says what is true and claims nothing about where the reader is standing: the
accounts are in the feed, and on no page.

### The two that render nowhere

Repaired to name the headline figure — a landmark, true from anywhere, which is the same repair the
parent finding made. They consequently leave the here-relative census. The alternative repair is a
door that renders `different_contrast_caveat`; that is a site change and is **not** done here.

## Why nothing else could have caught any of the three

All three sentences are on branches today's artefacts do not drive. No published feed carries them,
so the whole-site sweep had no string to judge; the producer sweep recorded them and declined to
refuse. Every existing control over this prose asked whether a sentence *was* here-relative. None
could ask **where it would land** or **what was at the other end**.

* **swap `#arms-realised` above `#arms-headline` in the door** → the direction leg reds on all six

from `_polarity_check`'s referent and agrees until one of them moves.
