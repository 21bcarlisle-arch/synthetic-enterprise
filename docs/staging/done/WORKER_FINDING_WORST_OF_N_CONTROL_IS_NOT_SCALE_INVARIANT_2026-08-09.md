# WORKER FINDING — a worst-of-N control gets stricter as N grows, and nothing declares its N

**Found:** 2026-08-09, building the C14 population half (`simulation/premise_population.py`).
**Class:** R15 (a control whose verdict depends on something other than the thing it judges).
**Disposition:** QUEUED as an atom. NOT fixed on sight — the supply of harness findings is
infinite and this is not blocking (SELF_INTERRUPT_DISCIPLINE).
**Owner atom:** `H_GAP_fabric_belief_truth_gap` / `W1_12_premise_trace_generator`.

## Observed, with evidence

The two-level test (`background/fabric_gap_ledger.py::evaluate_two_level`) is GREEN on the
authored 10-premise panel and RED on a drawn 200-premise population **generated by exactly the
same generator, on exactly the same weather, with the same seed**:

```
n=  10  L1.1=0.1573  L1.2=0.4114  L1.5=0.1000  red=False  failed=[]
n=  25  L1.1=0.1573  L1.2=0.4114  L1.5=0.1000  red=False  failed=[]
n=  50  L1.1=0.1573  L1.2=0.8595  L1.5=0.1000  red=True   failed=['L1.2']
n= 100  L1.1=0.1573  L1.2=0.8595  L1.5=0.1333  red=True   failed=['L1.2','L1.5']
n= 200  L1.1=0.0414  L1.2=0.8868  L1.5=0.1500  red=True   failed=['L1.1','L1.2','L1.5']
```
(first n of one drawn population, so the n=10 set is a subset of the n=200 set — the world is
not changing, only how much of it is being looked at)

Every L1 cell is a **worst-home** statistic — a max or min over the population. A worst-of-N is
monotone in N by construction: it can only move away from the band as homes are added. The
bands were anchored while the panel had ten homes, and `L1.2 at_most 0.85` / `L1.5 at_most 0.1`
encode "no home in TEN may exceed this", not "no home may exceed this".

## Why this is a control defect and not a world defect

`observed-with-evidence`: the degradation is monotone in N on a fixed population.
`inferred`: a real book has millions of premises, so the honest form of every L1 band is a
QUANTILE or a rate ("fewer than X% of homes exceed"), not an extremum. As written, the same
generator passes at n=25 and fails at n=200, and the report says nothing about which N produced
the verdict — a reader cannot tell a worse generator from a bigger sample.

Note this cuts BOTH ways and the fail-open direction is the worse one: at n=10 the control is
so weak that a genuinely bad generator would likely pass.

## What must NOT be done

Widening the bands so n=200 goes green. The bands are anchored (L1.1 to published TDCV/EST/DESNZ
arithmetic, 2026-08-09); loosening them to accommodate a sample-size artefact would destroy the
anchor to fix the wrong thing (R12). The fix is the STATISTIC, not the threshold.

## Proposed work (the atom)

1. Restate every L1 cell as a population rate at a declared quantile, with the quantile named
   and defended, and with N reported in the cell note.
2. R15 both ways: the restated control must still fire on the defect the extremum caught
   (mutate the generator toward smoothness), and must NOT fire merely because N grew.
3. Re-run W1_12's L3 evidence under the restated control. **W1_12's L3 promotion rests on a
   green two-level test measured on the 10-home panel**; whether it survives at population scale
   is currently unknown and is not claimed either way here.
