# FINDING — the here-relative vocabulary is an unbounded allow-list, and its gaps are silent

**Severity:** BLOCKING
**Lane:** `H_harness`

BLOCKING is the honest header even though the ten instances are repaired and landed in this same
turn. What is repaired is the instances; what remains is a detector whose coverage of its own
defect class is unknown and unbounded, and which reports that gap as a pass. No published figure
is wrong and none moved — the cost is that four purpose-built controls certified a live two-home
pointer as clean, and nothing in the tree can say how many more there are.

## What was landed in this turn

`_HERE_RELATIVE` in `site/test_a_here_relative_pointer_has_one_home.py` gained `run` on the noun
axis and `published` on the participle axis, and the ten live pointers that widening exposed were
repaired in `tools/generate_value_arms_data.py` by **naming their subject** — the same repair
`dc92d2d65` made for the figure pointers. The feed was rebuilt so the page carries the repair.

**One of the ten was a live instance of the parent defect, not merely an unjudged one.**
`_staleness_caveat` published *"re-running the noise floor on the run published above is owed
work"*, and that string renders in **two** regions — `/capabilities/#arms-errorbar` and
`/capabilities/#arms-headline`. A reader meeting it in the headline is sent somewhere the reader
meeting it in the error-bar block is not. That is exactly the two-home shape this family of
controls was built for, it was live on the deployed page, and every sweep on the site passed it.

## The finding proper, and it is not about these ten strings

**The vocabulary is an allow-list over an open class, so every gap in it is silent.** Three
separate things were measured on 2026-09-19:

1. **The noun axis cannot be closed by enumeration.** A census of deictic `the <noun> above|below`
   across `site/data/*.json` returns **70+ distinct head nouns**; eight were registered. The
   producers use *split, cut, bar, spread, width, reading, pass, evidence, estimate, comparison,
   bound, span, contrast, size, growth, withdrawal, refusal, advantage, decomposition, sentence,
   leg* and more, all in unmistakably deictic sentences. `run` was one word in a long tail.

2. **The participle axis had the same hole and nobody had looked at it.** Nine participles were
   registered (`shown|listed|set out|stated|named|given|described|printed|quoted`) and `published`
   was not — while `published above|below` was live **six times in one producer**. This gap was not
   in the drawn item; it was found only because the census was run by axis instead of by word.

3. **The obvious inversion is refused, and it was built before it was rejected.** "Match any noun,
   deny-list the comparators" re-admits `generate_value_arms_data.py:5371`, *"whether a larger
   settled book moves the interval above"*, where `above` is the direction a **number** moves —
   the counter-example `tests/tools/test_the_proof_pages_undriven_pointers.py:43` already had on
   the record. **The grammatical refinement fails too**, which is the new result: that string's
   `above` is followed by a finite verb (*"is NOT ESTABLISHED"*), so the noun-phrase test a reader
   would reach for passes it straight through. It is not that the regex is weak. *"The interval
   above"* is genuinely ambiguous out of context, and this file only ever sees it out of context.

## The remedy, named and deliberately not built here

Widening the list again next time a word is missed is the same turn repeated. The shape that would
actually bound it is a **partition, not a longer allow-list**: every live `the <noun> above|below`
in a producer must be either recognised by `_HERE_RELATIVE` **or** on an explicit exempt list that
states why it is not a page pointer (arithmetic, comparison, ranking). A new noun then lands as a
red naming its own string instead of as silence, and the exempt list is small, argued once, and
reviewable — which the noun list is not.

**Why it is filed rather than built.** It needs ~92 live strings in one producer classified one at
a time, each a judgement, and the classification is worth nothing if rushed. That is more than the
turn that found it can do honestly, and a half-classified partition fails open — the unclassified
remainder reads as exempt. The ten repairs and the widening stand on their own and are landed.

## What this cost, said plainly

Between `dc92d2d65` landing and this turn, the value-arms page carried a two-home here-relative
pointer, and four controls built specifically to find two-home here-relative pointers all passed
it. The repair in `dc92d2d65` was itself verified against a census this vocabulary built, so it
certified one pointer in a sentence clean while blind to the other pointer in the same sentence.
**A repair verified by a detector inherits that detector's blind spot, and inherits it silently.**
That is the generalisable half, and it is not specific to prose detectors.
