# The ledger fix reached origin on the reconciler's push, not on the landing (2026-10-01)

**Severity:** RECORDED · **Lane:** H_harness · **Epoch:** unassigned · **Atom:** `unminted`

**Premise spent.** `git merge-base --is-ancestor ebc9af681 origin/main` returns 0. Nothing was re-landed or byte-compared, because the commit itself, not a copy of it under another sha, is on origin.

**Which door wrote it, and whether that door pushes.** `ebc9af681` (parent `6a47d2108`) was written onto the shared tree's HEAD at 00:14 BST by `tools.surgical_land`, and `tools/surgical_land.py` contains no `git push`. The only occurrence of the word is the `_refuse_a_landing_no_ref_holds` docstring (line ~1515), which says a moved origin "makes the landing a non-fast-forward to push, which the reconciler reports". A surgical landing is therefore local by design. Publishing belongs to `background/origin_reconcile.py`, which runs `surgical_land --merge origin/main` in the isolated worktree `/var/tmp/se-origin-reconcile` (`_run_merge`) and then pushes it with `_push`, i.e. `git push origin HEAD:main`. From 23:42 to 23:57 UTC on 09-30, `reconcile-watch-log.md` records every attempt as `ERROR ... another writer holds /var/tmp/se-origin-reconcile` (16→18 behind, 1→2 ahead). The 00:22 UTC run logged "the merge gated clean and was pushed". That run is merge `fedfeea78` (01:22 BST, parents `b1b4c284e` local side + `8dc8dcd97` origin side), and `ebc9af681` sits on its first parent. So the commit went unpushed for about 70 minutes because the reconcile worktree was held, and the drawn item was minted around 00:23 UTC, right as that push landed. The same run's log also says the shared tree "did NOT advance" (refused by `tests/background/conftest.py`, modified here and on origin). So the shared HEAD is now 20 behind with 0 ahead. That is the open fork-alarm subject (`SEAT_FINDING_THE_ORIGIN_FORK_ALARM_WRITES_THE_FILE_THAT_BLOCKS_THE_FAST_FORWARD_IT_REPORTS_2026-09-30.md`), not this item's.

**For the land-twice row.** Until the reconciler pushes, a gated `surgical_land` commit on the shared HEAD is invisible to any reader that asks origin, and that is correct. The gap is bounded by the reconciler's cadence, plus any time its worktree lock is held. Re-landing during that window would duplicate the work. The remedy is to wait for the reconciler's `pushed` line, not to land again.
