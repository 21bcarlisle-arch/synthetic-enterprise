post-hoc one.

So, fixed in advance:

1. The fifteen is read **only if** the twelve are read and reported first, with their own mean,
   sem and sign.
2. It is reported **whatever it says** — including when it disagrees with the twelve, and
   including when it rescues a sign the twelve refuse. A fifteen computed and then not published
   because it was unflattering is the defect this section exists to prevent.
3. It is **not** wired into `NOISE_FLOOR_PATH` by this claim. Publishing it is a separate decision
   on separate evidence.
4. If any of the five identity rows above fails to hold on the delivered artefact — in particular
   if `redraw_key` or the world digest differs — the fifteen is **abandoned**, not repaired.
