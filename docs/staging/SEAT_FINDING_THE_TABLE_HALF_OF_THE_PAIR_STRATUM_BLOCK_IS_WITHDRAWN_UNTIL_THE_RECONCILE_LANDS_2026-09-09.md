# The TABLE half of the pair-stratum block is withdrawn, and it has to come back

`bec6071f7` deleted this lane's `pairStrataBlock`, `_skill_pair_strata` and
`_pair_strata_interval_clause` so that `39065da9d` could be a merge rather than a collision. This
is the record of what that cost, written at the moment it was paid rather than reconstructed later.

## Why the deletion happened

Two lanes built one director item — attribute the estimand's inversion to the tie mass or to the
arm — concurrently, each internally consistent and each with a green door control. **Git merges them
without reporting a conflict.** Measured on the merge tree, not predicted: two module-level
`def _skill_pair_strata`, two `"pair_strata"` keys in one dict literal, the later of each winning,
which pairs this lane's function with origin's call site and makes the block refuse on a feed that
attributes perfectly well. The gate refused that tree on `F601: baseline 1, now 2` plus five door
reds.

`surgical_land --merge` takes no `--content`, and `--resolve` is only for paths git itself reports as
conflicted. So a path that auto-merges WRONG cannot be settled inside the merge, and one side has to
reduce to the merge-base first. That is the whole of why a deletion was the move.

## Why this side lost, and it was not seniority

Origin's producer computes a real permutation over the cross stratum's own pairs
(`run_value_cycle_ab.cross_stratum_null_spread`) and **withholds** that stratum's rank when it
cannot, so no figure reaches a reader without the interval its own sample earned. This lane's copy
composed prose *explaining* the absence of an interval instead — a weaker answer to the same
question.

And it was about to be actively wrong. This lane's cell had two states: a number, or
"no comparable pair". On the live feed under origin's producer the cross stratum has **4,588
comparable pairs and a withheld rank**, so the cell would have printed *"no comparable pair"* beside
it — a false statement about the estimator, on the published page, from the moment the two met.
`SEAT_FINDING_THE_LANE_0_ITEM_WAS_BUILT_TWICE_...2026-09-09` predicted the collision and
`b01c77d16` made the interval CLAUSE conditional to survive it; neither of them saw that the TABLE
had the same defect one axis over. A conditional composed for the sentence does not protect the cell.

## What the reader lost, and it is not nothing

Origin's renderer publishes `pair_strata.reading` and nothing else. That sentence *asserts* "the tie
mass supplies NONE of the 12,194 comparable pairs and cannot move the figure". This lane's table was
the only place a reader met the three strata's own counts — 37 zero decisions, 666 pairs among them,
0 of them comparable — which is what makes that sentence a fact a reader can check rather than a
claim they must accept. **A published assertion whose supporting counts are only in the feed is a
figure published without its evidence**, which is the shape this page keeps paying for.
