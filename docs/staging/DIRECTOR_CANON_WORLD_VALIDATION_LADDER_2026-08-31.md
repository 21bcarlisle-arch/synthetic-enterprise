# [DIRECTOR-CANON] — The world validation ladder: bottom-up generation, top-down checks (2026-08-31)

**Type:** [CANON — a standing standard for how every world variable is built and validated. Director-authored intent, drafted with the advisor. This is a crux document: it explains a month of "we cannot tell" results and it governs everything the world does from here.]

**Mission link.** We create enterprise value by automating ways to find individual customers we can create value for, and sharing in that value. That requires a world in which individual customers genuinely differ, for reasons of their own, that a supplier could in principle infer. This document is how the world earns that property and how we check it holds.

---

## 1. The principle

**Every household carries its own behaviour and its own probabilities, derived from its own attributes with a hidden rationale.** Income, engagement, need for predictable bills, satisfaction with the current supplier, a home move, a bill shock, a better offer seen through some channel. The population is drawn with the intent that its aggregates land inside published ranges — but **no macro variable is ever set as a target.** Aggregates emerge from individuals. Published figures are checks the world must pass, never dials that set it.

**Why this is the crux.** For a month the company has been asked whether it can predict the world, and every answer has come back "we cannot tell." A world validated top-down — aggregates pinned to the record — contains no per-customer signal. Individual variation becomes noise around a forced mean. The company kept failing to find inference advantage because there was structurally nothing to infer. That was never a weak company; it was a world built as a mirror of the record instead of a mechanism that produces it.

**The one move that is always wrong:** clamping an aggregate to pass a check. It passes the test by destroying the thing the test exists to protect. When a check fails, the repair goes to the individual model — the attributes and the rationale — never to the aggregate.

**The safeguard against drift:** a bottom-up world with a wrong individual model can wander far from reality, and nothing top-down will pull it back. So the checks below have teeth. They are red tests, not advisory bands. Drift gets caught, and gets fixed at the individuals.

---

## 2. The ladder

Four rungs, in order. Each is a test. None is a target.

### Rung 0 — Red lines

A wide feasible range from published evidence. Outside it the world is wrong and the run fails. **Wide on purpose**: this rung stops crazy answers; it does not tune. A world that passes rung 0 is merely not absurd.

### Rung 1 — Level

Are customers roughly doing this, on average, over time, as the knowledge says they should? A check against the published record, with a band. The band is a *check* — the world's level is not set to sit inside it; it is measured against it. A world whose level sits on the exact edge of the band every year has almost certainly been fitted to that edge, and has no headroom to show a change in either direction. That is a symptom of top-down construction, and it is what the recent churn anchor exhibited.

### Rung 2 — Mechanism

Does the variable respond to its drivers the way we understand it should? The cap, the fixed-to-SVT spread, availability, a crisis, a price move by this supplier. **History is evidence for that understanding, not the understanding itself.** Fixed take-up falls when fixed prices sit above SVT because of a causal relationship we can state in words; 2022 is where we saw it, not why it is true.

This rung therefore holds beyond the record. When the world runs past 2025 there is no series to sit inside — but the same mechanism must produce the same shape of response to conditions history never contained. **That is what an anchor means once there is no history: a mechanism the world must obey, not a number it must match.** It answers the generative-futures question: the future is neither "preserve the statistical shape" nor "honestly unanchored" — it is anchored to mechanism, validated on history where we have it, and applied where we don't.

### Rung 3 — Heterogeneity

Do individual customers within the population make different choices, for hidden reasons of their own, that aggregate to the average? Are those reasons ones a supplier could in principle observe or infer through a channel it actually has? **This rung is the thesis itself.** Without it there is nothing for the company to infer, and every A/B result is an artefact. A variable that passes rungs 0–2 and fails rung 3 is a world that replays rather than lives.

---

## 3. How the rungs are used

- **Every world variable is checked against all four**, and each Knowledge page states which rungs its variable currently passes. A page that cannot say is a page whose variable has not been validated.
- **Rung failures repair downward, never sideways.** A rung 1 failure is fixed by correcting the individual model's attributes or rationale until the emergent level moves — not by scaling the aggregate. A rung 2 failure is fixed by correcting the mechanism. A rung 3 failure is fixed by giving individuals the rationale they lack.
- **Existing top-down anchors are debt.** Any world variable currently set as an aggregate target — including the churn anchor, and the stickiness fix of 28 August if it was done by scaling — is to be re-examined against this ladder and, where it fails, rebuilt from the individuals up. Say plainly which were top-down.
- **Independence is not inference.** Passing rung 3 establishes that there is something to infer. It does not establish that the company infers it. The two claims stay separate on every published surface, per the standing rule.

---

## 4. What this does not say

It does not prescribe mechanisms, distributions, or which attributes drive which behaviours — those are the delivery seat's, built knowledge-first from published evidence per the choice-and-channel brief. It does not set the width of any band; those come from the record. It does not require every variable to pass all four rungs today; it requires every variable to say where it stands.

Curriculum values — the population's marginals, how many households are highly elastic, how long the window runs — remain the director's, and are taken from published evidence rather than from what flatters a result.
