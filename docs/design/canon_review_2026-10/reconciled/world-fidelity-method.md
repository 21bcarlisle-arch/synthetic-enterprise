# world-fidelity-method: consolidated

Scope note. All 139 extracts read. The seven named canons are principles 5 (aggregates never targets), 6 (validation ladder), 9 (tails not means), 16 (synthetic book), 17 (demand vector), 18 (weather cells), 19 (half-hourly spine). Quotation marks are the director's words as the extracts carry them (R-B-2 and R-E-50 are advisor articulations). A standing-page "no" is the worst case across merged ids; brackets name what is on the page.

## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

1. **The simulation is not a model; fidelity is the precondition; the SIM leads.** "The simulation is not a model … a running approximation of the company itself." "Fidelity is not the goal — it is the precondition: a world that cannot press back cannot tell value created from value transferred." "The SIM leads and the company catches up."
sources: R-S-119, R-S-120, R-S-122 (June 2026), R-C1-70 (2026-08-24), R-F-40 (08-27), R-S-5, R-S-45, R-S-104 (08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (precondition and "defeat" sentences yes); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

2. **SURVIVE means not guaranteed to live; real history is the severity; no difficulty knob.** "The company does not have to die. It has to not be guaranteed to live" — through "cash and timing problems, not profit problems." "2022 is a fact, not a scenario"; "a difficulty dial will eventually be turned until the test returns the wanted answer."
sources: R-B-116, R-B-117, R-B-118 (2026-07-25), R-C1-117, R-S-35, R-S-108 (08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (record yes; C3 and knob rulings no); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

3. **R13: the baseline/curriculum split, the curtain, and ratified marginals.** "The world changes only for fidelity reasons, decided blind to company results. Which world the company lives through is the director's, named and versioned in a file he can read." "We want resilience and the capability to endure, not pot luck in one world"; "a change to any published marginal is a fidelity finding decided blind to P&L, never a side effect."
sources: R-S-83 (2026-07-12), R-A-19, R-A-89, R-B-104, R-S-110 (07-23), R-A-26 (07-27), R-B-158 (07-28), R-S-36 (08-28), R-D-68 (09-05), R-S-164 (10-11)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly

4. **Hard boundary: no personal microdata.** "Published aggregates and cross-tabs only. The ratified pitch stakes a public claim that no household data has entered the system; that must stay true."
sources: R-A-95 (2026-07-20)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

5. **Aggregates are never targets; generation is bottom-up.** "Every household carries its own behaviour and its own probabilities, derived from its own attributes with a hidden rationale … no macro variable is ever set as a target. Published figures are checks the world must pass, never dials that set it." "Clamping an aggregate to pass a check … passes the test by destroying the thing the test exists to protect … Rung failures repair downward, never sideways."
sources: R-A-20 (2026-07-23), R-B-143 (07-27), R-F-19 (08-17), R-C1-13, R-C1-14 (08-31), R-F-62 (09-03)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R12 generic only); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

6. **The World Validation Ladder: four rungs, each a test, none a target.** Rung 0 red lines; rung 1 level — "a world whose level sits on the exact edge of the band every year has almost certainly been fitted to that edge"; rung 2 mechanism — "a mechanism the world must obey, not a number it must match"; rung 3 heterogeneity — "this rung is the thesis itself." Checks "are red tests, not advisory bands"; "Generation and validation from separate sources."
sources: R-C1-13, R-C1-15, R-C1-16 (2026-08-31), R-F-62 (09-03), R-D-61 (09-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

7. **Not a segment, a person; heterogeneity expressed, not recorded.** "The bill payer, not the meter." "The person who doesn't pay their bill in February isn't necessarily a bad debt risk. They might have just had a baby." "Until a trait changes a behaviour, 'coupled' overstates what exists"; "a household that has never switched at any price is disengaged, not price-insensitive."
sources: R-S-121 (June 2026), R-S-135, R-S-136 (undated), R-F-42 (2026-08-27), R-C1-115, R-S-106 (08-28), R-C1-3 (08-30), R-D-57 (09-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

8. **Choice and channel is the missing mechanic; the market must contest.** "No competitor, no channel, no quote, and no reason attached to any departure … the company can never be right or wrong about why a household moved. That is the only thing worth inferring … This ranks with weather and wholesale." "'Beating the flat baseline' compares two internal policies in a market that could not react to either."
sources: R-C1-116, R-C1-119, R-S-111 (2026-08-28), R-C1-1 (08-30)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-C1-116 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

9. **Tails, not means — in scoring and in sampling.** "The fidelity score is the worst-explained cell of that grid, not the population average." "Annual averages hide tails. Measure it at the extremes." Sampling is tail-heavy — "a world's unlikeliness discounts its EV contribution but never excuses dying in it" — scored as ROBUSTNESS, COMMERCIAL EV and SURVIVAL, never conflated.
sources: R-B-1, R-B-3, R-B-12, R-B-15 (2026-07-19), R-A-103, R-B-108 (07-23), R-B-112 (07-25), R-A-68 (07-27), R-D-58 (09-05), R-F-105 (09-07), R-F-119 (09-09)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

10. **Anchored, evidenced, believable.** "Every assumption is registered against an external source, and where the simulation diverges from a published benchmark, the divergence is recorded rather than tuned away." "A viewer over data nobody recorded is worthless." "Does it feel like the real UK market to a 20-year veteran"; "Theorise where the physics is solid … Don't theorise where it's behaviour."
sources: R-B-1, R-B-2, R-B-3, R-B-4, R-B-15 (2026-07-19), R-E-7 (07-22), R-A-7 (07-28), R-D-61 (09-05), R-F-110, R-F-111 (09-08)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-A-7, R-E-7, R-F-110 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (R-A-7 partly)

11. **No interim tuning; the naive baseline is frozen; the lift table is the scorecard.** "No interim tuning … no 'temporary' recalibration while the structural work waits." The baseline is "FROZEN and independent — Goodhart applies the moment lift becomes a target"; an aggregate lift "may not be stated anywhere without the per-cell truth beside it."
sources: R-B-3 (2026-07-19), R-A-83 (07-25), R-B-143 (07-27), R-B-158 (07-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (two partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (two partly)

12. **Fusion and coupling: hypotheses with refutation conditions; declared independence.** "Conservative crossing by default … An honest gap is recoverable; a confident error is not." Assumptions are "TESTABLE HYPOTHESES", each stating "what observation would refute it"; "no trait pair may be drawn independently without a register entry saying so."
sources: R-A-93, R-A-94, R-B-38 (2026-07-20), R-A-89 (07-23), R-D-68 (09-05), R-F-98 (09-07)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

13. **Gap triage: measuring a gap is the cheap outcome; faced-or-scheduled; conflicts are findings.** "A simplification the harness has never been exposed to is an untested assumption, not a proven scope choice"; "Reclassification to make a red disappear is a claim-status defect"; "surface the conflict as a director finding, do not silently resolve."
sources: R-B-57 (2026-07-22), R-A-102 (07-23), R-A-41, R-A-42 (07-28), R-C1-116 (08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-A-41 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

14. **Measurement habits, and the tie-breaker.** "Before measuring a thing, say what it is." "Before dividing two numbers, say what each one counts." "A prediction filed after the answer is not a prediction." "'Identical to four decimals' is the tell … Check the mechanism rather than the number." "Don't build until you've told me the shape." Tie-breaker: "erring against our own thesis is the safe direction."
sources: R-F-44, R-S-12, R-S-14, R-S-15, R-S-19 (2026-08-27/28), R-F-96 (09-06), R-F-97 (09-07), R-F-109, R-F-110 (09-08), R-F-118 (09-09)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (the four R-S habits yes); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

15. **Independent reconstruction: arithmetic checked only against itself is not checked.** "The remedy is not more tests written by the same hand. It is independent reconstruction … every disagreement is a finding about the biller." Six properties stand, among them "the validator wins by default"; "say plainly where this residual risk sits."
sources: R-C2-19 (2026-08-12), R-D-20, R-D-21, R-D-22 (09-02)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-C2-19 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

16. **The synthetic-book canon: generated from a fitted joint, drawn for difference.** "The sample is modelled houses, each placed in a coherent weather cell, with a coherent set of people living in it. Real data calibrates and checks it; real data is not the thing we select from. So NEED … is evidence, not population." "Fresh draw per life, never a fixed library. The fitted joint is the asset; the houses are not." "Draw for difference, not for proportion"; "Coverage is a property of the ensemble of runs."
sources: R-B-34 (2026-07-20), R-A-68 (07-27), R-D-57, R-D-60 (09-05), R-D-38, R-D-40, R-F-105 (09-07)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-D-57 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

17. **The demand-vector canon: a household is a vector, not a number.** "A household is a vector, not a number, and the sample has to span the range of those vectors across the stock, including the tails, not match their averages." "Cases are CHOSEN for difference, and the weights carry the representativeness … if N comes out at the scale a random sample would need, the weighting is doing no work." "Report both numbers — N to reproduce the observed distribution and N to also span intervention response — and treat the second as the real one." "Refuse any coverage, ceiling or sufficiency claim that doesn't declare the dimension it reduces over."
sources: R-D-31, R-D-32, R-D-35, R-F-100, R-F-102, R-F-104, R-F-106 (2026-09-07)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-F-106 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (R-F-102 partly)

18. **Weather cells and one shared world.** "Divide the UK up into clusters of similar primary weather drivers. Same temp vs mean, windchill, same solar … the maximum level of simplicity that can be achieved whilst still capturing fidelity for the vast majority of households." Persistence and "cross-cell synchrony (when Glasgow is cold, is London?)" in phase 1; "Validation is independent of the weather data." The count is "derived rather than designed"; twenty regimes only "if it holds at the tails." "Two households in the same cell must experience identical weather — that's what makes the difference in their demand attributable to fabric and people … That's not an optimisation, it's what a simulation is."
sources: R-D-74, R-D-75 (2026-09-05), R-F-93 (09-06), R-F-102 (09-07), R-F-115, R-F-116 (09-08), R-F-118, R-F-119 (09-09), R-D-90 (09-16), R-D-91 (09-20)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-D-91 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

19. **The half-hourly spine stays half-hourly; per-household shape is the deliverable.** "A daily grain cannot price a time-of-use or dynamic tariff, cannot express imbalance exposure, and cannot measure load shifting — which is the whole carbon thesis. GB settlement is half-hourly and that is not an implementation detail, it is the market. So the half-hourly spine stays half-hourly … put a control on it." "Per-household shape is not an enhancement to the demand work; it is the deliverable." "For gas the resolution is needed for the PHYSICS."
sources: R-C1-75 (2026-08-24), R-D-33, R-D-37 (09-07), R-F-109, R-F-110, R-F-111 (09-08)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

20. **Model the fundamentals; generate the residual with honest tails and regimes; the weather world is never tame.** "Model the fundamentals; generate the residual as a calibrated stochastic process with honest tails and regime behaviour; never pretend to forecast the unforecastable." "A single-regime mean-reverting model is disqualified by our own decade record"; power must be "reconstructible from gas, carbon, demand and wind on ordinary days, or it was not formed, only generated". "A stationary, tame weather world makes every downstream claim soft."
sources: R-A-99 (2026-07-22), R-A-102, R-A-103, R-B-87, R-B-101, R-B-102, R-B-103 (07-23), R-B-143 (07-27)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (R-A-103 partly); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

The commercial layer follows" (§3). "The rated-versus-in-use gap is modelled, not ignored"; "Charts diagnose and communicate; numbers prove"; "Heat pump is out of all three phases until the director says otherwise."
sources: R-D-58, R-D-61 (2026-09-05), R-D-72 (09-06), R-D-34 (09-07)

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. **Stage-1 order.** R-D-34 (2026-09-07): "The physical layer is what stage 1 needs. The commercial layer follows." The merit-order amendment R-D-56 (2026-09-05, cited in R-D-34's note) put commercial behaviour into People P1, and that is what is being built (2026-10-11) while W2_19 sits at L0. A ruling would need to say which order stage 1 follows.

2. **Coverage across the ensemble vs power inside one run.** R-D-57 (2026-09-05): "Coverage is a property of the ensemble of runs … not every house in every geography in one run." R-F-106 (2026-09-07): "I'd rather carry thousands and know why", and the 2026-10-07 "few thousand customers" steer (cited in R-D-57's note) want power inside one run. A ruling would need to state which N serves which.

3. **Fresh draw per life vs a fixed founder book.** R-D-60 (2026-09-05): "Fresh draw per life, never a fixed library." R-S-155 (2026-10-10) authorises "40-founder books" from a fixed `FOUNDER_BOOK.yaml`. A ruling would need to decide whether founders are curriculum (R13) on a freshly drawn world, or the library the ruling forbids.

4. **World-first vs the 10-10 balance.** R-S-104 (2026-08-28): world-must-press "outranks new capability"; R-F-40 (2026-08-27): "Don't over-polish the company against a world we already know is thin." The director's "2026-10-10 balance" (DIRECTION, cited in R-S-104's note) lets world items wait when they block no supplier result. A ruling would need to say which governs.

5. **Tell-me-first vs investigate-and-record.** R-F-10 (2026-08-13): "Tell me before any published figure moves." R-F-64 (2026-09-04, other theme) replaces asking with investigate-and-record. A ruling would need to state which applies when a published figure moves.

6. **Three fixed scores vs an "open" fitness function.** R-B-112 (2026-07-25) fixes ROBUSTNESS / COMMERCIAL EV / SURVIVAL; DIRECTOR_CANON §6 still lists the Epoch-4 fitness function (A5) as open. A ruling would need to say whether R-B-112 closes A5.

7. **Ratified engagement bins vs the continuous-propensity ask.** R-A-19 / R-S-110 (2026-07-23) ratify 0.45/0.35/0.20 as marginals that "must not move as a side effect" (R-D-68); R-A-21 (2026-07-23) asks for a latent, state-dependent propensity, and MOAP TF2 reads "continuous engagement replacing the three bins." A ruling would need to say what the ratified marginals become.

8. **SERL: "not pursued" vs "go looking".** R-D-37 / R-F-102 (2026-09-07): "Don't pursue SERL … mine to take." R-F-111 (2026-09-08): "Go looking for half-hourly gas — smart meter datasets, SERL, any published trial." A ruling would need to say whether that licenses an access application.

9. **No household data vs the volunteer test bed.** R-A-95 (2026-07-20): "no household data has entered the system … must stay true." R-A-94 (same document) tests hypotheses on real households via "the shadow-billing volunteer track." A ruling would need to say whether consented volunteer data sits outside the claim.
