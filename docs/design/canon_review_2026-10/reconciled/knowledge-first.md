# knowledge-first: consolidated

Dates are 2026, MM-DD. R-S-9 (08-28) is the CLAUDE.md rewrite date, not the ruling's; R-S-50, -51 and -56 share that date. Standing-page checks are greps of CLAUDE.md, DIRECTOR_CANON.md and OPERATING_MODEL.md. "Partly" means the older core is on the page and the later or specific part is not.

## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**1. Knowledge first is a rule.** "A number you need is a question to research, never a value to pick": published sources, then discovery against what we have built, then a roadmap. Where the record runs out, "say so rather than filling the gap with a plausible number"; the code "carries the gap explicitly rather than a placeholder that looks like an answer."
sources: R-S-56 08-28, R-C1-2 08-30, R-F-55 08-30, R-S-10 08-30, R-C1-105 08-31; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md yes; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**2. Omission is not neutrality.** Where physics or strong logic makes a relationship near-certain, "model it as a stated belief rather than omitting it. Omission is not neutrality — it asserts zero, and zero is usually the one value we know is wrong." "A parameter we can move is worth more than a number we're confident in."
sources: R-F-112 09-08 (corrects an over-application of 1); on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (lines 63-65 teach the uncorrected rule); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**3. Unpublished means a ranged toggle.** "Where the truth isn't published, make it an assumption toggle: a parameter with a plausible range, and test whether the answer changes across it. If it doesn't change any decision, the question doesn't matter. If it does, dig harder — and only bring it to me if it's both decision-changing and unresolvable." Ranges come from published data, "labelled as estimates"; no a priori weights, and an unavoidable one is "chosen, declared, and sensitivity-tested". For the world, "a labelled range, not a number."
sources: R-A-97 07-20, R-L-39 10-05, R-L-49 10-08, R-L-55 10-09, R-L-59 10-11, R-L-62 10-11; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (no "toggle" anywhere on it); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**4. Three sides of knowledge, the practitioner last.** Published evidence, discovery, and "a practitioner for what's obvious to anyone in the industry and therefore never written down": an odd reading, "say so and ask rather than building on it." But his corrections are "the practitioner starting point, not the answer"; "I'm not giving figures"; "When a question touches regulation, search Ofgem's licence conditions and decisions before asking me"; "A question to me comes only after that page shows the answer isn't published."
sources: R-D-11 09-01, R-L-40 10-06, R-L-49 10-08, R-L-55 10-09, R-S-163 10-10/11, R-L-59 10-11, R-L-60 10-11; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (09-01 text only, lines 90-96; "Ofgem" appears nowhere on it); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**5. Scope first, from the domain.** Write the scope brief "FIRST, independently of what we hold": "what must a complete treatment of this topic contain, for a reader who needs to understand it?" Then assemble; "The DELTA between brief and assembly is the primary finding"; "Blind where possible." Market and cost logic is discovered from real sources, not handed over: a sketched chain "becomes a specification."
sources: R-B-14 07-19, R-A-52 07-28, R-A-54 07-28; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (knowledge-first, not the method); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**6. The agent owns the design; his guesses are hypotheses.** "This document is a starting point, not a specification. The agent should own the design." "If a question can be settled by running something, run it." "My guess, to test rather than assume"; "If one of mine is wrong, say so."
sources: R-S-144 undated, R-S-9 08-28, R-L-06 09-26, R-L-07 09-27, R-L-22 10-03; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (R-S-9 verbatim; "hypotheses under test", line 174; design ownership absent); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**7. Knowledge is the product.** "a project whose value is the domain": understanding "lands in the Knowledge layer with its sources and its gaps stated"; "Findings about the machine go to the registers." A canon document that establishes understanding reaches the layer like a research document. Write for outsiders: "an outsider can learn something rather than check us"; "the honest version is more persuasive than a clean one." Owed pages get written; parked work is written up so its findings survive.
sources: R-C1-6 08-30, R-F-79 09-05, R-F-107 09-07, R-L-21 10-02, R-L-27 10-03, R-L-36 10-04; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (it says read the layer, never the write rule); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**8. Knowledge before build.** "Before building or changing any supplier capability, write or amend the relevant knowledge page first: what the rules require, what published practice and evidence say, what's unknown (labelled GAP), and which choices the design makes and why. Publish it on the site, and cite the page in the build commit." Retrospective for the live save and the payment plan. Three areas needed "proper knowledge work, not a re-check of a toggle value", then toggles set "from what you find."
sources: R-D-6 09-01, R-L-40 10-06 (staged as R-D-46 10-07), R-L-45 10-07, R-L-60 10-11, R-D-84 10-11, R-L-61 10-11; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (the rule, lines 56-59; not page-first, publish, or cite-in-commit); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**9. A page's shape and place.** "what the published evidence establishes, what it does not, what we have assumed and why, and what would settle it." "named gaps live in the residuals rung, never in the body"; too little understanding, "ship a STUB." Topics split on one test: "Shared → one topic. Different → separate topics with a typed edge." "Amend an existing page wherever one fits; start a new one only when nothing does." Make it "impossible for a page's headline to disagree with its title."
sources: R-A-51 07-28, R-A-53 07-28, R-C1-6 08-30, R-L-21 10-02, R-L-45 10-07, R-L-49 10-08, R-L-60 10-11; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**10. Staleness and claim status are visible.** "Every page carries a last-reviewed date, and flips to a visible 'review due' state past a threshold." The badge separates "written, awaiting check" from "stub, never touched"; a director check against source moves a page to verified, checking route noted. A figure the pitch cites "must be derivable from primary state at publication time."
sources: R-A-3 07-28, R-C2-43 08-17, R-C1-21 08-18; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (canon_drift_check, line 234; no review-date rule); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**11. The published record governs the numbers, definitions matched.** Fetch real rates "rather than extrapolating, and mark anything still extrapolated as such on the surface that shows it"; "the commons wins — treat the doc as the origin of the question, not the authority on the answer"; marginals "from published evidence, not from what makes the arm look good", and if little can be inferred, "that's the finding." Use a published series, not a rebuild. "Before measuring a thing, say what it is." Published outcome rates are "a reality check on the world rather than an input", compared "with definitions matched"; complaints are "a floor on dissatisfaction, not a measure of it."
sources: R-F-14 08-14, R-C1-25 08-18, R-F-41 08-27, R-D-10 09-01, R-L-36 10-04, R-L-39 10-05, R-L-49 10-08, R-L-50 10-08; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (R-D-10 verbatim, lines 80-87; the rest implied or absent); DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (commons doctrine, line 14)

**12. Constants carry their origin; simplifications are recorded.** "Refuse unsourced domain constants at the door": each such constant in company/ and saas/ carries a citation, "a labelled belief the company holds and something grades", or "a named simplification with what it would take to do properly"; "Same name may not carry two values anywhere"; a cited money constant must be reached. "Simplify wherever necessary — but record what was simplified, what it would take to do properly, and what it would change if done." The seat asks "what else assumes it, and does that assumption still hold."
sources: R-S-50 08-28, R-S-51 08-28, R-C1-5 08-30, R-F-56 08-30, R-C1-105 08-31; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (rules table, simplifications home; not "two values"; no roadmap habit); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**13. Knowledge is the machine's to get on with.** "Get on with, without asking: knowledge, in-flight work, error fixing, and correcting plain factual errors anywhere, including canon. Say what you corrected." Step-1 knowledge starts "straight away and in parallel, since it doesn't collide with anything."
sources: R-L-28 10-04, R-L-37 10-05, R-L-44 10-07; on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (line 151; the priority order is unnamed); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; OPERATING_MODEL.md yes

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

**A. Build while a question is open?** R-D-11 (09-01; CLAUDE.md 95-96): an odd reading, "say so and ask rather than building on it." R-L-28 (10-04; CLAUDE.md 156-158, 186): "An open question to me sits in a list and never blocks the queue"; "Silence is validation: notify and proceed." October (R-L-39, R-L-60) narrows when to ask, not what the build does meanwhile. A ruling would decide whether a decision-changing, unresolvable question holds that piece or work proceeds on the labelled range.

**B. Which designs wait for his go?** R-S-144 (undated) "propose any changes to the model design via NTFY before building"; R-L-48 §5 (10-08) "bring a proposal, don't change it yet"; R-L-59 §5 (10-11) "Design, then bring it to me before building into the run" (ruled on, R-L-64, 10-12). Against: R-L-28 (10-04) "Escalate with a proposal, and don't wait"; R-L-57 (10-10) directs the reactive save built live; R-L-60 (10-11) makes the page the gate. A ruling would name the test: every new supplier capability, designs touching something he ruled, or only those he names.

**C. Publish before build, or weekly?** R-L-60 (10-11) wants the page published "on the site" and cited in the build commit. R-L-06 (09-26) anchored publishing to "Monday after the reset"; R-L-29 (10-04) calls it "weekly now"; R-F-107 (09-07) had a finished page stuck "unlanded — site lane red". A ruling would decide whether knowledge pages are exempt from the weekly cadence, builds wait for Monday, or "publish" means merged to the site source.

**D. Where does a GAP go?** R-A-51 (07-28): "named gaps live in the residuals rung, never in the body." R-L-45 (10-07) asks for "GAP" marked "where nothing is published" on existing pages; R-L-60 (10-11) lists "what's unknown (labelled GAP)" among a page's contents. As written, placement differs. A ruling would decide whether a GAP label may sit inline beside its claim or only in the residuals rung.
