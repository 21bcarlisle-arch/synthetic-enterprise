Dates are 2026 (MM-DD). R-L ids are the director's console words (October log). R-S-124 and R-S-145 to R-S-150 are undated vision text, not quoted director words. Page status was grepped on CLAUDE.md, DIRECTOR_CANON.md, MOAP (THE_MODEL_ON_A_PAGE.md) and OPERATING_MODEL.md.

## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**1. Margin, and every output, is a diagnostic, never a target.** "R12 anti-goal-seek — margin is a diagnostic, never a target", generalised to "Outputs are diagnostics, never targets".  
sources: R-S-82 (07-12), R-S-44 (08-28)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md yes; DIRECTOR_CANON.md yes

**2. Value is created, then shared; the score is still one-sided, and making it two-sided is his call.** "Charging someone the cap transfers value rather than creating any"; "Nothing optimises the household figure ... until the director decides it is, which is R13". Scoring both sides of the value split was ordered. See conflict 1.  
sources: R-S-101 (08-28), R-S-102 (08-28), R-S-109 (08-28), R-C1-94 (08-28)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (line 19 states the two-sided mission; the reservation is only in MOAP); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**3. A performance claim must pass the wall, beat a baseline and carry its null.** "The advantage must come from inference, never from access"; the control is a supplier "applying flat rules with no per-customer view". A belief-vs-truth gap is "a measurement, never evidence of skill", never stated without the null interval; say "we cannot tell". Arms that lose different customers hold different books, so compare decisions ("Same person, same conditions, two rules"). An arm narrower than the world it is scored against bounds every A/B figure. Report founders and wins separately, or show the mix does not change the answer.  
sources: R-C1-83 (08-25), R-C1-102 (08-31), R-L-25 (10-03), R-L-26 (10-03), R-S-112 (10-04), R-L-58 (10-10)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (the wall is on both)

**4. The pool is not the book; figures carry their N, draw and clock.** "Fourteen customers cannot populate twelve segments"; volume-dependent capabilities stay "untestable". "The company's book is what it earned, never what it was granted." No cohort-derived £ figure leads a page. "I don't take financials as useful at all. Certainly not totals from a random sample of customers": show £ per customer, rates and distributions with N.  
sources: R-B-92 (07-23), R-A-40 (07-24), R-A-28 (07-27), R-A-66 (07-27)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**5. Knowledge first; unpublished truth is a toggle with a range; the page precedes the build.** "Knowledge first", not "a constant chosen because a number was needed"; "a sourced number that exists and isn't wired should not be able to sit quietly beside an unsourced one that is." Where truth isn't published, "make it an assumption toggle: a parameter with a plausible range", and bring it to him only if "both decision-changing and unresolvable" (but tell him when a result turns on a number). Write or amend the knowledge page first, publish it, cite it in the build commit; search Ofgem's rules before asking him. Customer-chosen behaviour with no published take-up runs only as sensitivities, "never as findings".  
sources: R-C1-97 (08-28), R-C1-98 (08-28), R-L-39 (10-05), R-L-49 (10-08), R-L-55 (10-09), R-L-59 (10-11), R-L-60 (10-11), R-S-162 (10-11)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (only the knowledge-first core, line 56); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**6. Factual corrections go ahead; changes to what he ruled come as a proposal; some numbers stay his.** "Where a gap is real, fix it as a factual correction, unless the fix changes something I ruled, in which case bring me the proposal", then re-grade what depended on it (the flat 0.20 retention modifier was "a factual correction, not a choice for me"). His: the founder ceiling and prospect cap ("Those two numbers stay my decision"), payment-plan design before it enters the run, the CLV discount rate and terminal-value method.  
sources: R-C1-98 (08-28), R-E-57 (09-05), R-L-46 (10-08), R-L-50 (10-08), R-L-51 (10-09), R-L-52 (10-09), R-L-59 (10-11)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (general rule, line 151); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**7. World first, freeze, then solve; truth is an instrument.** "The world creates customer behaviour behind the curtain, from evidence. The supplier sees only what a real supplier could see." Build the household side first from research, with no reference to supplier policy, carrying a labelled range where evidence is missing. File predictions and freeze the world before testing any supplier solution; change it later only on new evidence, saying so. Supplier lanes read only through the seam and are graded on fresh seeds across the uncertainty ranges. The decision probe and holdout truth are for grading, "never inputs to a supplier decision".  
sources: R-L-62 (10-11), R-L-64 (10-12)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (the wall and seam alone are on both)

**8. Segment on learning value, gated by decision-linkage.** "Value makes no sense. Bad debt is negative value but working out how to deal with them is really valuable." Rank segments by "which segments teach the company the most — not which customers are profitable"; "a segment scores value only if a decision is conditioned on it" ("Do not un-wire, hold, or re-park the gate"); protection is a floor outside the ranking. Cohorts are combinations of needs, attitudes and behaviours; seek the fewest segments covering the valuable variance and critical small groups (knee about 12 cells). Value arbitrates which gaps to close (gap × marginal cost of being wrong).  
sources: R-B-13 (07-19), R-A-96 (07-20), R-B-29 (07-20), R-B-30 (07-20), R-B-33 (07-20), R-E-6 (07-22), R-A-30 (07-23), R-B-99 (07-23), R-B-179 (07-29)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**9. The CLV merit order: Pays, then Stays, then the margin bet, only then value-add; pricing must see who pays.** Value is contribution × survival, so the drivers are "lexicographic, not additive": where contribution is negative, a longer life multiplies the loss. About people: "credit risk, rental vs owner, number of people versus typical for house" first, value-add last. "Good management of renters and won't pay (but can) can create as much value ... as other value add." Pricing was blind to payment history, "the first rung of my CLV merit order": forward arrears and credit risk to it and let arrears move bad-debt cost as well as churn.  
sources: R-D-53 (09-05), R-D-65 (09-05), R-E-55 (09-05), R-E-56 (09-05), R-D-70 (09-06), R-L-22 (10-03), R-L-26 (10-03), R-L-27 (10-03), R-L-57 (10-10)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**10. CLV done properly (vision text).** Three horizons (expected, actual, re-forecast continuously); systematic variance is model error, reported as pricing accuracy by cohort. Customers above marginal cost contribute; below-marginal retention needs explicit justification; actions are expected-value choices against do-nothing, "not rules"; return on capital is what risk governance optimises.  
sources: R-S-124 (June), R-S-145, R-S-146, R-S-147, R-S-148, R-S-149, R-S-150 (undated)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**11. Next best action is one per-customer decision: their benefit first, then ours, judged by CLV.** "Debt management is a form of next best action", as are retention, upsell and carbon advice; not "a fixed ranking of measures". Win-win, carbon-led and money-led mapping is its basis. Complaint statistics are "a floor on dissatisfaction, not a measure of it"; "model the silent unhappy". Build order: reactive save, home-mover retention, then debt as NBA.  
sources: R-L-37 (10-05), R-L-38 (10-05), R-L-39 (10-05), R-S-158 (10-06), R-L-57 (10-10), R-S-162 (10-11)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**12. Retention is save and win-back on the loss notice, plus home movers; blanket discounts are not the lever.** "A retention offer is usually margin sacrificed, not a cash payment." Rates come from the switching process's own data as toggles with ranges, "a reality check on the world rather than an input"; test three shapes (blanket cut, timing-targeted fix, reactive save) and say if the answer turns on a number.  
sources: R-C1-97 (08-28), R-L-48 (10-08), R-L-49 (10-08), R-L-50 (10-08), R-L-52 (10-09), R-L-57 (10-10), R-L-60 (10-11)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**13. Cost is not loaded onto a group "because it is less able or less likely to push back".** Turning customers down on payment behaviour, credit or arrears is ordinary practice; shifting cost onto prepayment customers is not. A save offer may vary by acquisition route or time on the default tariff; renewal prices may not; and it "must never be paid for by raising the price of customers who stay". Vulnerable customers fare "at least as well as anyone else in the same position".  
sources: R-L-49 (10-08)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

**14. Provision honestly; debt follows the person.** The provision-to-realised-loss variance is measured and published, never tuned away: "a company that believes it provisions perfectly is a wider hidden gap wearing a better number." Estimate on billed, outcome on settled, cash on banked; no bad-debt figure without its clock. Practitioner starting points, not answers: near-zero recovery after write-off, realistic debt-sale prices, back-billing liability by occupant. Misattributed debt is a customer-harm event.  
sources: R-B-127 (07-25), R-B-129 (07-25), R-B-133 (07-25), R-L-40 (10-06), R-D-47 (10-07), R-L-45 (10-07), R-L-55 (10-09)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no (R14, the clock rule, is the exception)

**15. Payment, credit risk and forward cash forecasting are "the heart of this business": one engine on one forecast.** Debt management merges with a personal payment plan per customer, to "design them as one". A forward cash forecast per customer drives the provision (expected loss per customer, replacing the flat assumption), balance limits, the plan, early warnings and self-serve responses where "some money steadily beats none". "Credit risk sets how far a customer's balance may drift from zero, not how much they pay over the year." A fixed DD is reassessed when new information would change it materially (SLC 27.15). Purchasing, DD setting, unbilled estimation and customer value share one consumption forecast, the best against held-back actuals; "CLV and pricing must read the same assumptions". Supplier-controlled levers first; the portfolio ceiling on credit held stays.  
sources: R-D-47 (10-07), R-L-59 (10-11), R-L-61 (10-11), R-S-162 (10-11), R-L-64 (10-12)  
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no

sources: R-B-58 (07-22), R-B-59 (07-22), R-B-60 (07-22), R-B-99 (07-23), R-B-105 (07-23), R-B-106 (07-23)

Acquisition spend driving collateral demands is "the first thing that would make SURVIVE mean something". Registered, not ruled: the growth/leverage session (R-B-120) and survival as a dose (R-B-114); see section 4.

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. **Is the objective two-sided yet?** Reserved: the score "has no household-side term", and nothing optimises the household figure "until the director decides it is, which is R13" (R-S-102, R-S-109, 08-28). Since: NBA is "their benefit first, then ours" (R-L-39, 10-05); the engine is scored with bill shock, credit held, complaints and hardship counted (R-L-59, 10-11); C34's joint-value ranking with a no-loss floor on both sides stays open (R-S-158, 10-06). A ruling would need to say whether "their benefit first" is the R13 decision or only the NBA ranking key, and whether the no-loss floor is ratified.
2. **Merit order: build sequence or decision rule?** "Lexicographic, not additive" (R-E-56, 09-05, advisor's wording) and "levers in merit order" (R-L-57, 10-10), against NBA "judged by CLV, rather than as a fixed ranking of measures" (R-L-39, 10-05). A ruling would need to say whether Pays-first is a strict rule inside every decision or only the order capabilities are built.
3. **What does "value per segment" score?** Learning value, "not which customers are profitable" (R-A-96, R-B-30, 07-20), against the efficiency axis, "value per segment (cost-to-serve vs value ...)" (R-E-6, 07-22). A ruling would need to say which metric the efficiency axis scores.
4. **Is company-side segmentation testable now?** It "stays marked untestable ... and unlocks only when acquisition becomes a decision variable at the director's growth session" (R-A-28, R-A-67, 07-27), against "we really need to work on new segmentation" (R-B-33, 07-20). An acquisition organ now exists, with a prospect cap he sets (R-L-46, R-L-48, 10-08); no growth session is recorded. A ruling would need to say whether the mark lifts at the current book or waits for the session.
5. **Which capital does return on capital use?** "Capital deployed = hedging margin requirements + bad debt provision + working capital" (R-S-147, undated), against cash and risk managed "within Ofgem's capital rules" under a portfolio ceiling on credit held (R-L-59, 10-11; R-L-64, 10-12). A ruling would need to say whether customer credit and Ofgem's capital target sit inside "capital deployed".
6. **Cross-subsidy.** "Visible, deliberate, and time-limited" (R-S-124, R-S-148, undated), against "cost is not loaded onto a group because it is less able or less likely to push back" and no save offer paid for by stayers (R-L-49, 10-08). A ruling would need to say whether any deliberate cross-subsidy survives beyond save offers.
