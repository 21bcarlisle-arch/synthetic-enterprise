## 1. Standing principles (what still governs)

**Carbon is one of three currencies, not demoted.** "Carbon is not demoted — it is one of three." "Every carbon commitment on this page (E5, the SAVED/SPENT/NET ledger, the £/tCO₂e front-page target) survives intact and keeps its level; what it loses is the claim to be the whole purpose."
sources: R-S-100, R-C2-34 (both 2026-08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (carbon is one currency; the not-demoted sentence is absent); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP yes

**What carbon is for: showing customers what they have saved and could save.** "Its main job is to show customers what they've saved and could save in CO2, so those motivated by it can enjoy it. That clarifies the mission's carbon part rather than changing it: we save customers cash, carbon and convenience."
sources: R-L-38 (2026-10-05)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (the mission names carbon, not this purpose); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**Savings are usage-side only; never discounting.** "Customer money-saving is declared the engine of abatement: bills cut by reducing or time-shifting usage, never by discounting." "The customer-savings ledger counts usage-side savings only — price transfers never count." "A discount can be gamed and a kilowatt-hour cannot." Save and retention offers are price transfers, so the rule bites on them (R-A-4 note; R-L-48 §3: "Blanket renewal discounts are not the lever").
sources: R-A-4 (2026-07-28), R-L-48 (2026-10-08)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (only "Transfer is not creation"); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**The score is a diagnostic, kept in three ledgers.** "£ per tonne of CO₂e saved = (cost to serve + cost to persuade, including compute) ÷ (carbon abated)", against £273/tCO₂e (2025). "It is a diagnostic, not a target — the moment a number becomes something to optimise, it stops measuring anything." "Three ledgers, all reported: carbon saved for customers; carbon spent serving them — people, compute, tokens; and the net position. A carbon claim that counts only one side is not a claim."
sources: R-A-10 (2026-07-28), R-S-100 (2026-08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (test_carbon_not_a_target; no yardstick or ledger named); DIRECTOR_CANON.md partly (R12); MOAP partly (no £273)

**Carbon is published with its provenance and says what it leaves out.** "Publish it with its provenance, and say plainly what it does and does not yet include." State now: household emissions are measured (NESO half-hourly intensity times reads); "the ledger is still called by nothing, and abatement is still unmeasured, because nothing supplies the counterfactual it needs."
sources: R-C1-81 (2026-08-25), R-S-113 (2026-10-09)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (still "designed and unwired"); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP yes

**Intensity: published history, modelled futures, correlated is enough.** "NESO publishes half-hourly carbon intensity for GB, and it's what a real supplier would use for any customer offer. I'm happy with a correlated estimate — it doesn't need to be precise." History: align the published series to settlement; if it "doesn't cover 2016, fill the gap simply from Elexon's fuel mix and standard emission factors rather than modelling dispatch." Futures: "fit a statistical model of carbon intensity on that history — wind and solar on the grid and demand will carry most of it", so future carbon stays consistent with future weather. EP13 is parked: "we'll return to it if first-principles carbon is ever needed."
sources: R-D-73 (2026-09-06), R-L-36 (2026-10-04 23:20)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no (NESO only in a data list); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP partly (line 198 uses NESO's series; no futures model, no EP13)

**Carbon knowledge is balanced, tested, and feeds next best action.** "I'm concerned our carbon knowledge leans electricity-centric ... For a typical gas-heated home, heating may be around five times the carbon of all its electricity." Test: an EV is a large total saving but raises household electricity carbon, "which a supplier can't net off because it can't see the petrol"; solar is "now mostly a money decision"; "a heat pump is often carbon-led". Map measures as win-win, carbon-led or money-led, built "as knowledge with sources and gaps". R-L-39 amends: "The goal is tailored next best action per customer — their benefit first, then ours", judged by CLV, "rather than as a fixed ranking of measures".
sources: R-L-37 (2026-10-05 02:24), R-L-38 (02:54), R-L-39 (11:39)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md no; DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

**Status disputed: the carbon score and products beyond price are out of scope "for now".** "Deliberately out of scope for now, by director decision: the carbon score, and products beyond price (efficiency advice, tariff fit, solar, heat pumps, time-shifting) ... They return once the basics hold." R-L-36 and R-L-38 evidently reopen carbon knowledge and measurement (see 2 and 3).
sources: R-C1-120 (2026-08-28)
on standing pages: CLAUDE.md partly (records "unwired", not the descoping or its return condition); DIRECTOR_CANON.md no; MOAP no

## 3. Unreconciled conflicts (needs the director)

1. Scope. R-C1-120 (2026-08-28) keeps the score and products beyond price "out of scope for now ... once the basics hold"; R-L-36 (2026-10-04) and R-L-38 (2026-10-05) direct carbon intensity work and a measures map. A ruling would need to decide whether "the basics hold", and whether the wired score and those products are back in scope or only knowledge and measurement.
2. Where carbon's priority rests. R-C1-81 (2026-08-25) "the entire point of this project"; R-S-100 (2026-08-28) one of three, keeping the "£/tCO₂e front-page target" (TF2 still says "Carbon becomes the headline"); R-A-10 (2026-07-28) "a diagnostic, not a target"; R-L-38 (2026-10-05) main job is the saved and could-save display. A ruling would need to decide whether £/tCO₂e still headlines the front page or sits behind the customer display.
3. Wording. The mission says "money, time and carbon" (R-S-100, 2026-08-28; CLAUDE.md adds "Time does not exist here at all"); R-L-38 (2026-10-05) says "cash, carbon and convenience". A ruling would need to decide whether convenience is time, and which wording is canonical.
4. Open, no rival position. R-S-157 (2026-10-05) proposes T&D losses "as its own named line beside the electricity figure, at DESNZ's published T&D factor ... never folded into the grid series"; until ruled, the page says losses are not included. It "never reached this list"; no October entry rules on it; a ruling would need to decide whether to add the line.
